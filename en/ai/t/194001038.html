Removing

Refitting

- Open the rear tailgate.

Op. 7040B26 LOAD PLATFORM COVER BLIND - R+R

- Release the two flexible belt mountings securing the protective grille to the mounting hooks.

- Release the upper sliding mounting points

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Release the side sliding mounting points and remove the protective grille from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Insert the side sliding mounting points in their housings.

- Insert the upper sliding mounting points in their housings.

- Attach the two flexible belt mountings securing the protective grille to the mounting hooks.

Op. 7040B26 LOAD PLATFORM COVER BLIND - R+R

- Close the rear tailgate.
