Connector

2. Undo the nuts (2a) and remove the accelerator pedal with built in potentiometer (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the accelerator pedal with built in potentiometer back in its housing and secure it using the nuts.

- Connect the electrical connection to the accelerator pedal with built in potentiometer.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
