L010 CLIMATE CONTROL NOT COOLING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fan works properly and sends air to the passenger compartment according to the pre-set flows but the air remains at the outside temperature and is not cooled.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

CHECK FOR GAS LEAKS FROM COMPRESSOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine started, the climate control system at the maximum speed (compressor ON button), place the temperature adjustment knob/button in the maximum cold position, move the "nose" of the leak detector near the connector to be analyzed and check for any leaks. If the result is positive (leak), repeat the test to make sure that there really is a leak. With the engine switched off, move the "nose" of the leak detector near the area of the compressor shaft and the area of the compressor rear gasket. If the result is positive (leak), repeat the test to make sure that there really is a leak.

Move on to Step 4

1.O-ring puckered.

1.Replace the o-rings and refill correctly.

Op. 0010T84 COOLANT FOR AIR CONDITIONING SYSTEM - DEHYDRATE AND REPRESSURIZE

2.Leak from compressor shaft.

2.Replace the compressor

Op. 5040A20 AIR CONDITIONING COMPRESSOR - R+R - INCLUDES FLUID LEVEL CHECK AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

3.Leak from compressor rear gasket.

3.Replace the compressor

Op. 5040A20 AIR CONDITIONING COMPRESSOR - R+R - INCLUDES FLUID LEVEL CHECK AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY
