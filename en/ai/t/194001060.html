Removing

Refitting

- Open the tailgate.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Description

Connector

BATTERY

See A001 BATTERY

Op. 7040G46 LOAD COMPARTMENT COURTESY LIGHT - R and R

1. Release the retainers (1a), lift up the protection (1b) and extract the bulb (1c) from the courtesy light in the load carrying compartment (press-fit).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check the condition and the cleanliness of the bulb.

- Fit the bulb in the courtesy light for the load carrying compartment (press-fit).

- Lower the protection and attach the retainers.

Op. 7040G46 LOAD COMPARTMENT COURTESY LIGHT - R and R

- Connect the negative battery terminal.

Description

Connector

BATTERY

- Check that the bulb is working.
