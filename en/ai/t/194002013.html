The customer complains of an excessive rolling and/or pitching sensation of the vehicle, especially at the back.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

ROAD TEST TO EVALUATE THE ROLLING/PITCHING

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out a road test to evalute the folling/pitching of the vehicle

See Test 44B ROAD TEST FOR ROLLING/PITCHING EVALUATION

End of diagnosis

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 6

Acceptable behaviour of the vehicle

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 5.5

Move on to Step 2

Subjective judgement compared with the reference vehicle 3-4

Move on to Step 4

2

END OF TRAVEL BUFFER CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the end of travel buffer for the rear spring is not faulty carrying out

See Test 4420AA CHECK ON REAR SUSPENSION SPRING END OF TRAVEL BUFFER

Move on to Step 3

End of travel buffer deteriorated

Replace the end of travel buffer

Op. 4420W14 REAR SUSPENSION END BUFFER - REPLACE

3

SHOCK ABSORBER EFFICIENCY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the efficiency of the shock absorbers by carrying out manual compression manoeuvres (resting on the wheel arch by the centre of the wheel), which should be carried out on both sides of the vehicle.

Move on to Step 5

Sudden return without damping

Replace the shock absorber that is not working properly

Op. 4420A30 REAR SUSPENSION SHOCK ABSORBER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. - CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

4

VEHICLE GEOMETRY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

After replacing the shock absorbers, check the geometry

End of diagnosis

Geometry values do not conform

Restore the geometry to the correct values

Op. 4450A40 REAR WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT, INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY
