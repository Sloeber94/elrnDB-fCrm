PRELIMINARY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the level of the engine coolant is within the recommended limits and that the coolant is recirculating correctly in the system. Check that there are no constrictions in the water pipes to the heater.

Move on to Step 1

1. Engine coolant level too low.

1.Restore the correct level checking that there are no leaks from the system.

2. Coolant does not circulate correctly (thermostatic valve not working properly).

2.Refer to the fault diagnosis

See table K025 ENGINE COOLANT TEMPERATURE GAUGE NOT WORKING

3. Water pipes constricted.

3.Repair the water pipes.

1

CHECK ON CORRECT CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine on and the compressor off, place the control unit to the AUTO function with the temperature knob at the maximum hot HI position.

Also select the recirculation button to the outside air intake position and set the fan speed to 3-4 bars (display) and the distribution to front ventilation. Using the Examiner, check that the engine temperature has reached at least 70°C.

There is a noticeable variation in temperature (getting hotter).

End of diagnosis

The temperature does not appear to get hotter.

Move on to Step 2

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check that there are no errors:

  • at the climate control node;
  • at the engine temperature sensor;
  • at the passenger compartment temperature sensor;
  • at the outside temperature sensor;
  • at the mixture actuator;
  • at the treated air sensor.

Move on to Step 3

1. Move on to Step 4

2. Refer to the fault diagnosis

3. Error at the passenger compartment temperature sensor.

3. Move on to Step 4

4. Error at the outside temperature sensor.

4. Move on to Step 4

5. Error at the air mixture actuator.

5. Move on to Step 4

6. Error at treated air sensor.

6. Move on to Step 4

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner diagnostic equipment connected, check the operation of the air mixture actuator through active diagnosis.

Move on to Step 4

Air mixture actuator not working

Replace the air mixture actuator

Op. 5040D77 AIR CONDITIONER MIXER FLAP CONTROL MOTOR - R R

4

CHECK ON CLIMATE CONTROL NODE ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine switched off, remove the control unit from the dashboard mounting and disconnect the cables connector from the control unit visually inspecting for any damage and reconnect the connector correctly to check for any false contacts. Check the electrical connections at the outside temperature sensor and the mixture actuator.

Move on to Step 5

1.Electrical connections loose or damaged.

1.Refit the electrical connections correctly.

2.Outside temperature sensor not working.

2.Replace the outside temperature sensor

Op. 5010D12 OUTSIDE AIR TEMPERATURE SENSOR ON DOOR MIRROR - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

3.Passenger compartment temperature sensor not working.

3.Replace the climate control system control unit

Op. 5040D18 AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

4.Treated air sensor not working.

4.Replace the treated air sensor

Op. 5040D18 AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

5. Air mixture actuator not working

5.Replace the mixture actuator

Op. 5040D77 AIR CONDITIONER MIXER FLAP CONTROL MOTOR - R R

5

AIR MIXTURE ACTUATOR FLAP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect that the air recirculation actuator flap completes its travel without any hinderance.

End of diagnosis

1. Flap travel incomplete.

1.Free from air recirculation actuator flap from the impediment.

2. Flap damaged.

2. Replace the flap

3. Kinematic mechanism damaged.

3. Repair kinematic mechanism.
