5010C57 LEFT LOWER AIR DIFFUSER - R R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Working on the lower left hand side of the dashboard, undo the fixing bolts (1a) and release the vent (1b) from the mounting points under the dashboard. Then release the vent from the climate control casing and remove it from the dashboard.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Fit the vent on the climate control casing, checking that the seal is correctly fitted.

- Position the edges on the mounting points and tighten the fixing bolts.
