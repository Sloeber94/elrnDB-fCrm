Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

If the engine oil is being replaced, resetting operations must be carried out using the Examiner.

Engine oil is harmful to skin: keep contact with skin to a minimum and if there is contact wash with soap and water.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Unscrew the oil drain plug.

Collect the engine oil in a suitable container.

- Tighten the new gearbox oil drain plug to the recommended torque.
