Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK THE CORRECT FASTENING OF THE ELECTRIC MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the bolts fixing the motor are correctly tightened

Move on to Step 2

Incorrect tightening of the bolts.

Restore correct tightness with a torque of 2 Nm.

2

CHECK THE CORRECT OPERATION OF THE SUNROOF MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation of the sunroof operating motor.

In particular, check:

  • engine pinion condition;
  • control rack condition.

End of diagnosis

Faults in motor components.

Move the sunroof to closed position and remove the motor. Refit the new motor in the same position avoiding misalignment between the two bowden cables, replace the motor.

Op. 7030B78 SUN ROOF BLIND MOVEMENT MOTOR - R.R.
