7030B18 SLIDING ROOF GLASS PANEL - R+R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Place the sun roof (1a) preselector in the second opening position and place a soft spacer (1b) for supporting the glass and do not scratch it.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) on the rear part of the sun roof and the bolts (2b) on the front part and remove the transparent section.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Position the transparent section and tighten the bolts to the front section of the sun roof.

- Tighten the bolts to the rear section of the sun roof.

- Check that the sun roof is working properly.
