Removing

Refitting

Check that the components connected are not distorted by checking the bodyshell alignment measurements using suitable equipment (gauges, templates or jigs).

- Straighten the body as necessary.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Working on both sides, undo the fixing nuts.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the crossmember (2b) from the bodyshell.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Position the crossmember (1a) and screw in the fixing nuts (1b) without tightening them.

- Align the crossmember correctly and tighten the fixing nuts.

2. Tighten the retaining bolts (2a) to the recommended torque.
