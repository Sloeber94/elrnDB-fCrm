CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Use a Remote Examiner to check that the version on the engine management control unit is the latest

Carry out Flash Eeprom programming of the engine management control unit

2

Repeat Flash Eeprom programming. Ensure it is successful.

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

3

Check the fault has disappeared

Replace the engine management control unit
