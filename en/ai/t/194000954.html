Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

When working on the compartment covers, follow these precautions:

- When working on the hinges, take care not to damage the paintwork.

- After an adjustment to a door that has involved moving the hinges, the paintwork must be renewed in the hinge area.

- Work on upholstery compents with care and use the required tools correctly to prevent damage.

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition key OFF and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

Always move the covers with the help of an assistant.
