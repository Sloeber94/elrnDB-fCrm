Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The current socket / cigar lighter P050 receives a direct supply from the battery via the line for relay T12 protected by fuse F52 both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B002.

The rear current socket P052 also receives a supply from the line controlled by relay T12 protected by fuse F44 both housed in the junction unit under the dashboard B002.

For versions equipped with the FIX & GO kit on the other hand, both the current socket / cigar lighter P050 and the rear current socket P052 receive a supply from the line protected by fuse F44 of junction unit B002.

The lighting for the cigar lighter P050 is supplied by the Body Computer M001 pin 18 of connector H.

The lighting for the current socket P052 is supplied by the Body Computer M001 pin 40 of connector E.

See E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING
