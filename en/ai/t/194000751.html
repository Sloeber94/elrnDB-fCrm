Removing

Refitting

Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B06 LEFT REAR WHEEL - R.R

1. Undo the fixing nut (1a) and release the end (1b) from the housing on the potentiometer arm.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the front headlamp alignment corrector potentiometer (2b).

3. Disconnect the electrical connector for the headlamp alignment potentiometer.
