1060GL CHECK OPERATION OF MAIN ENGINE CONTROL UNIT RELAY

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Turn the ignition key OFF.

In the engine compartment junction unit, remove relay T9 (in the middle of the lower row)

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 85 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is less than 0.3 V.

Move on to Step 4

Move on to Step 2

2

If faults are found, restore the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the engine compartment junction unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the wiring if necessary

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

and move on to Step 3

3

Check the fault disappears.

The test can be considered over

Repair the original junction unit and replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

4

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is less than 1.0 V.

Move on to Step 15

Move on to Step 5

5

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the engine management control unit connector A.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

6

Turn the ignition key OFF.

In the engine compartment junction unit, remove relay T9 (first on the right in the bottom row)

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T19 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

7

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the EGR solenoid

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the EGR solenoid valve

Op. 1080C24 E.G.R. SYSTEM SOLENOID - R + R

8

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the flow modifying solenoid valve.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the solenoid valve

Op. 1064A34 TURBOCHARGER PRESSURE ADJUSTMENT VALVE - R.R.

9

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the temperature sensor and fuel heating resistance

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T19 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the fuel heater

Op. 1044D10 FUEL HEATER - R + R

10

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the built-in throttle body actuator.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the throttle casing actuator

Op. 1060G50 THROTTLE BODY - R.R

11

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom from the brake pedal switch.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is greater than 1.0 V.

Replace the brake pedal switch

Op. 5550D10 BRAKE LIGHT SWITCH - R+R

12

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the battery positive

Turn the ignition key ON.

Measure the voltage between Pin 87 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth, ensuring it is less than 1.0 V.

Move on to Step 14

13

If faults are present, restore the wiring between the battery positive and the turbine variable geometry control solenoid

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the wiring if necessary

The test can be considered over

Replace the solenoid valve

Op. 1064B42 SOLENOID CONTROLLING TURBOCHARGER VANE POSITION ACTUATOR - R.R

14

If faults are present, restore the wiring between the various disconnected components

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the wiring if necessary

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

15

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the engine management control unit connector A.

Check the resistance between Pin 85 of the base unit for relay T9 and the earth is greater than 500 kOhm.

Move on to Step 17

Move on to Step 16

16

If faults are found, restore the wiring between the engine compartment junction unit and the engine compartment junction unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the wiring if necessary

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

17

Check that the resistance across Pin 85 of the base of relay T9 and Pin 72 of control unit connector A

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is less than 5 Ohm.

Move on to Step 16

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
