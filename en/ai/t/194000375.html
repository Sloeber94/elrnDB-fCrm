Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Working underneath the vehicle, undo the bolts (1a) and remove the front pipe protection (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts and remove the rear pipe protection.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the bracket (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a), loosen the bolt (1b) and turn the underbody reinforcement (1c) sideways.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the intermediate fuel vapour pipe, front fuel vapour pipe side.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Disconnect the rapid connector for the intermediate fuel vapour pipe, rear fuel vapour pipe side.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Remove the intermediate fuel vapour pipe after having released it from the underbody fastenings.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the intermediate fuel vapour pipe in its housing and secure it to the underbody using the clips.

- Connect the rapid connector for the intermediate fuel vapour pipe, rear fuel vapour pipe side.

- Connect the rapid connector for the intermediate fuel vapour pipe, front fuel vapour pipe side.

1. Place the underbody reinforcement (1a) in its housing and secure it tightening the new bolts (1b) and (1c) to the recommended torque.
