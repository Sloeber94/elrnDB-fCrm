Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

- Rather than only loosening the driven toothed pulley fixing bolts, as indicated in

Op. 1032B10 TOOTHED TIMING BELT - R + R FOR TIMING ADJUSTMENT OR REPLACEMENT

completely undo the pulley and remove it.

- Refit the toothed driven pulley in its seat and secure with the bolt without tightening.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

- On this version it is not possible to remove/refit the toothed driven pulley on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then work at the bench, customising the engine service procedures for this operation.
