Refitting

Before removing and refitting the Air Bags the following preliminary operations must be carried out.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

- Disconnect the negative and positive battery terminals and insulate them.

- Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

FOLLOW ALL THE RECOMMENDED SAFETY INSTRUCTIONSOp. 5580C AIR BAG SYSTEM

Several of the CAN nodes, including the air conditioning control unit M070 and the instrument panel control unit E050 nodes are programmed with default settings that the customer will find when they acquire the vehicle. If the air conditioning and/or instrument panel control unit is replaced, the Parts Dept. will send a pristine component. once fitted, the data memorized in the body computer must be transferred to it by carrying out the "PROXI ALIGNMENT" procedure using the Examiner.
