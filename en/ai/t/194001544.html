Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: RIGHT HAND DRIVE

Radio P020 is supplied directly (pin 4 of connector A) from the battery via the line of fuse F23 of the engine compartment junction unit B001 .

Pins 1 and 3 of connector A are served by the CAN network that connects the radio to the Body Computer M001 (pins 24 and 25 of connector H) and to the other system nodes. Pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

Radio P020 is connected via the CAN network to the radio controls on the steering wheel integral with steering wheel control unit M093 - pins 1 and 5 - via clock spring junction D047 (pins 3 and 4).

The aerial P25 is connected to the radio by means of the special coaxial cable.

Connector B of P020 requires connection to the speakers:

- pins 3 and 4 to driver side front speakers P035 (mid-woofer) and P045 (tweeter);

- pins 5 and 6 to passenger side front speakers P036 (mid-woofer) and P046 (tweeter);

- pins 7 and 8 to the left rear speaker P040,

- pins 1 and 2 to the right rear speaker P041.

The connection lead to CD-Changer P075 leaves connector E.

The connection leads to the telephone wirin leave connector D.

See E3580 PREPARATION FOR CELLULAR TELEPHONE
