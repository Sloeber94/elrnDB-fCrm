Refitting

The injection-ignition system control unit is a component that is sensitive to electrostatic interference. Do not touch the injection-ignition system control unit once the engine is started unless the static electricity has been discharged first by touching the bodyshell.

1. Disconnect the electrical connections for the injection/ignition system control unit.
