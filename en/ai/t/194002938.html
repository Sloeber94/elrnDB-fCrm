The diagnostic equipment signals: Error U1711 (CAN (NCM – NCA)).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods; only with equipment: AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION

This error is detected if the control module detects a fault in the automatic transmission system.
