Removing

Refitting

The following procedure describes the removing-refitting of the right front suspension anti-roll bar joint, the same operations should be carried out for the left front anti-roll bar joint.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

1. Undo the nuts (1a), (1b) and remove the front suspension anti-roll bar joint (1c).

Use a spanner on the facings on the joint for counter-torque.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Place the front suspension anti-roll bar joint in its housing and secure it tightening the nuts (1b) and (1c) to the recommended torque.
