Applies to: all engines; all production periods

For versions produced up to 12/2005

For vehicles with annual mileage of less than 30,000 km (e.g. about 15,000 km) there is an Annual Inspection Plan with the following contents:

- Check condition/wear of tyres and adjust pressure if necessary (including spare wheel);

- Check operation of lighting system (headlamps, direction indicators, hazard warning lights, luggage compartment, passenger compartment, glove compartment, instrument panel warning lights, etc.)

- Check windscreen wash/wipe system operation

- Check windscreen/rearscreen wiper blade position/wear

- Check front disc brake pad condition and wear

- Check cleanliness of bonnet and boot locks, cleanliness and lubrication of linkage

- Visually inspect condition of: engine, gearbox, transmission, rigid and flexible pipes (exhaust - fuel system - braking system) rubber elements (boots - hoses - bushes - etc.)

- Check battery charge status

- Visually inspect condition of accessory drive belts

- Check and, if necessary, top up fluid levels (engine cooling, brakes, windscreen washer, battery, etc.)

- Replace engine oil and oil filter (petrol versions - Multijet versions without DPF)

- Replace engine oil and oil filter (Multijet versions with DPF)

- Replace pollen filter.
