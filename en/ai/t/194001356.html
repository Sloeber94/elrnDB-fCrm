Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The fog light activation signal reaches pin 35 of connector G of Body Computer M001 from a control key on the left panel H091 (pin 1 of connector A).

The dipped headlight activation signal reaches pin 28 of connector G of Body Computer M001 from stalk unit H005.

See E2011 DIPPED HEADLAMPS

The fog light activation signal reaches pin 1 of connector I of Body Computer M001 from stalk unit H091.

See E2030 FOG LIGHTS

The signal that the key inserted and turned ON in the igntion (INT) reaches underfacia junction unit B002 (pin 11 of connector H) and from here passes to Body Computer M001 (pin 9 of connector F).

Body Computer M001 controls activation of the rear fog lights respectively:

- from pin 31 of connector E for the left rear fog light (mobile part on boot) F035;

- from pin 20 of connector D for the right rear fog light (mobile part on boot) F036;

Via the CAN line, Body Computer M001 (pins 5 and 6 of connector I) connects to instrument panel E050 (pins 3 and 4 of connector A), to manage the rear fog lights activated warning light.
