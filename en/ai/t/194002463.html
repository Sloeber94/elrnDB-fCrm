Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The Trip Computer in the instrument panel shows the following information on its display:

General Trip:

- Distance travelled [km] or [miles]:

- Instant consumption [l/100 km] or [mpg]:

- Average fuel consumption [km/l] or [l/100km] or [mpg]

- Range [km] or [mpg]:

- Average speed [km/h] or [mph] :

- Journey time [hh:mm] :

Trip B (partial):

- Distance travelled B [km] or [miles]:

- Average fuel consumption B [km/l] or [l/100km] or [mpg]

- Average speed B [km/h] or [mph] :

- Journey time B [hh:mm] :

The values indicated by the letter B are calculated from the last reset (SET key pressed on stalk lever to the left of the stalk unit).

For more details,

See descriptions 5560 INSTRUMENTS

If the car is fitted with a navigation system, all the information is repeated on the display.

Trip Computer data is calculated inside the instrument panel.

The instrument panel is a CAN node through which information is exchanged with the Body Computer and with the other electronic units.

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL
