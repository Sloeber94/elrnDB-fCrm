2. Brake pedal switch faulty

2. Replace the brake pedal switch

ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT CONNECTIONS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the ignition key in the ON position and the brake pedal pressed, check for a voltage signal at the engine management control unit coming from the brake switch:

See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL

Move on to Step 3

Repair the engine connections

See E5060 CRUISE CONTROL
