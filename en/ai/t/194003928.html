1064BE CHECK TURBO VALVE PROTECTION FUSE

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Reconnect any disconnected components

Turn off all the electrical consumers.

Turn the ignition key ON.

In the under-dashboard junction unit base

insert a new fuse F35 (second from the right in the upper row) and check it cuts in.

Move on to Step 3

A temporary current overload has occurred in the system behind fuse F35. move on to Step 2

2

If faults are present, restore the correct operation of the under-dashboard control unit wiring.

Replace it if necessary.

The test can be considered over

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A18 MULTI-FUNCTION CONTROL UNIT - R R

3

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom from the brake pedal switch.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Check that fuse F35 does not cut in

Move on to Step 6

4

Connect a shunt cable with a fuse between sensor Pin 4 of the switch

See I030 BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

and the battery positive

See A001 BATTERY

Check the brake lights do not come on

Replace the brake pedal switch

Op. 5550D10 BRAKE LIGHT SWITCH - R+R

5

If faults are present, restore the correct operation of the wiring between the engine control unit and the brake pedal switch

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace if necessary. The test is complete

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

6

Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the temperature sensor and fuel heating resistance

Turn the ignition key ON.

Check that fuse F35 cuts in.

Replace the fuel heater.

Op. 1044D10 FUEL HEATER - R + R

7

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the air flow meter.

Turn the ignition key ON.

Check that fuse F35 cuts in

Replace the flow meter

Op. 1060G10 AIR FLOW METER - R.R.

8

Check and restore the correct electrical connections between the engine compartment junction unit and components tested

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Replace the wiring if necessary

Replace the junction unit

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.
