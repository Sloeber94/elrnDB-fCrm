Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The parking sensor control unit M084 receives a supply at pin 1 of connector A from the ignition-controlled INT line protected by fuse F49 of junction unit B002; pin 8 of connector A is earthed.

The control unit is connected, via pins 7 and 4 of connector A and the CAN, to all the other nodes in the same network; in this way it receives, amongst others, the reverse gear engaged signal.

It sends the commands for operating the buzzer to the instrument panel via the CAN.

A system operation inhibition signal reaches pin 5 if a trailer is connected via coupling D073.

See E2050 TRAILER WIRING

The four parking sensors K097 are connected to connector B of control unit M084. Pin 1 supplies a reference earth, pin 6 a power supply, pins 5, 3, 2 and 4 are connected to sensors K097E, K097F, K097G and K097H respectively.
