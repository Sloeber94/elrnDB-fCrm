E2530 INSTRUMENT LIGHTING - FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Supply line INT (key ON) protected by fuse F49 of the under-dashboard control unit B002, sends lighting to tunnel control unit H036, hazard light control H020, left control panel H091 and manual air conditioner M070 - pin 28 -.

The front door control lighting H044 and H050 is managed by driver side door control unit M066 and passenger side unit M067, supplied - at pin 11 of connector D - from a line protected by fuseF32 of B002 and connected - pin 15 and 16 of connector B - via the CAN network to Body Computer M001.

Similarly, the lightng of the window controls on the left rear door H053 and right rear door H054 is managed by the boot control unit M063, supplied - at pin B8 of connector A - by a line protected by fuse F36 of B002 and connected - pins A7 and B7 of connector B - via the CAN network to Body Computer M001.

Body Computer M001 receives the side lights on signal at pin 28 of connector G.

See E2010 SIDE LIGHTS / NO. PLATE LIGHTS

The side lights on power supply is supplied to Body Computer M001 respectively:

- from pin 18 of connector H. to the radio wiring, to phone wiring D099 and to front cigar lighter/power point P050;

- from pin 40 of connector E. to electrochromic interior mirror P065 and rear power point P052.

The “MODE”, “MODE +” and “MODE -” controls from left control panel H091 send analogue signals to instrument panel E050, pin 16 of connector B and pin 9 of connector A.

Instrument panel E050 is supplied by an ignition-operated supply (INT from the steering lock control unit) - pin 1 of connector A - from a line protected by fuse F37 of under-dashboard junction unit B002 and directly from the battery - pin 2 of connector A - via a line from fuse F53 of B002. Pin 7 of connector A is earthed.

Via the CAN line, Body Computer M001 is connected to instrument panel E050, from which it receives a brightness adjustment signal that is sent to the radio or Connect Nav+ node P020, automatic climate control unit M070 and steering wheel node M093.

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES
