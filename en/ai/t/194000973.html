Refitting

Removing

1. Use appropriate equipment to cut and level the sealant strip to leave a thickness between 0.25 and 1 mm in the roof housing compartment.

The thickness of the sealant remaining in the roof housing will act as a support for the new bonding.

Thoroughly clean the roof housing compartment on the body with compressed air and degrease thoroughly with heptane

When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the windscreen to allow the primer thinner to evaporate.
