5040DG MIXTURE ACTUATOR CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Disconnect the electrical connector for the climate control system control unit

See M70 CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

With the ignition ON, turn the distribution knob to the bi-level (completely to the left).

After about 10 secs. turn the knob to the feet position (completely to the right) checking that the voltage between pins 1 and 21 is between 9.6 and 14.4 V whilst the motor is turning.

Repeat the manoeuvre in the opposite direction.

Also check that the voltage between pins 13 and 7 is between 4.75 and 5.25 V.
