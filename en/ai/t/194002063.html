Whilst driving round bends (both left and right) there is a noise in the area of the gearbox.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Steer to the right or to the left and check whether the noise increases.

Steer fully and then release the steering wheel: check whether the intensity of the increases or not with the steering angle.

Move on to Step 1
