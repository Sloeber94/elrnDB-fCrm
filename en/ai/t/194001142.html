Refitting

Op. 7050A20 ONE-PIECE REAR SEAT CUSHION - R.R.

1. Undo the front bolts fixing the backrest.

Tilt the backrest forward.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo nut (1a) on the bolt joining the right backrest (1b) to the left backrest (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Unscrew both rear nuts (1a) securing backrest (1b).

Remove the seat backrest from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the seat backrest is not damaged.

- Place the seat backrest in position in the vehicle.

- Fold down the seat backrest.

- Screw in the two rear nuts fixing the backrest and tighten them to torque.
