- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Open the bonnet.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Connector

BATTERY

Op. 7040A26 STEERING COLUMN TRIM - R+R

1. Unscrew and recover bolts (1a) from the control panel junction trim (1b), then remove the trim.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Unscrew bolts (1a) securing the retaining panel (1b) and partly prise up the control panel from the facia to gain access to the electrical connection.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect electrical connection (1a) and remove the control panel (1b) from the facia.

Description

Connector

1a

INSTRUMENT PANEL

See E050 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The control panel cannot be disassembled. It can only be removed and refitted to the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Several of the CAN nodes, including the instrument panel, are programmed with default settings that the customer will find when they acquire the vehicle. If the panel is replaced, the Parts Dept. will send a blank component. once fitted, the data memorized in the Body Computer must be transferred to it by carrying out the "PROXY ALIGNMENT" procedure using the Examiner.

- Connect the electrical connection to the control panel.

Connector

- Tighten the bolts fixing it to the retaining panel.

- P osition the control panel junction trim and tighten the bolts.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
