Removing

Refitting

In the following procedure the removing-refitting of the left rear suspension anti-roll bar joint is described, carry out the same operations for the right rear suspension anti-roll bar joint.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).
