Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The front seats have a special heating system, consisting of two heater elements (pads) located inthe cushion and in the backrest that allow the seats to be heated when the temperature conditions are very harsh.

The seat heating device, which can only be activated if the key is inserted in the igntion device and turned to ON (INT line) can be switched on using the special control in the control panel.

The various controls in the switches I065 and I066 adjust the temperature of the seat; an internal temperature sensor, with a feedback function, guarantees that the temperature set is maintained.
