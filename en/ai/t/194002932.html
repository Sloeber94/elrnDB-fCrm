The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2226 (atmospheric pressure sensor), with detail "signal plausibility"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if the engine speed is below 50 rpm and the difference between the supercharging pressure and the barometric pressure sensor is more than 0.15 bar for at least 1 sec. As a result, the control unit carries out the calculations with a replacement value of 0.751 bar.
