1. Working carefully, release the sun blind (1b) through the slot (1a) in the panel and release it from the retaining profile (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the sun blind is not damaged and the retaining profiles are intact.

- Introduce the sun blind carefully through the slot in the panel.

- Fit the sun blind on the retaining profile.

- Refit the retaining clips and tighten the fixing bolts.
