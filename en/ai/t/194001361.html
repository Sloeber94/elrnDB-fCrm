Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Pin 1 of connection on the vehicle for connecting trailer D073 receives power directly from a line protected by fuse F36 from the underfacia control unit B002.

This power supply crosses the boot compartment control unit M063 (from pin B8 of connector A at pin B7 of the same connector).

Via pin 2, it sends an inhibition signal to pin 5 of connector A of parking sensor control unit M084.
