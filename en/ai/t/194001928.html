Applies to: all engines; all production periods

SPECIFICATIONS

The front and rear bumpers are made from polypropylene and contain impact absorbing buffers.

The wheel housings are protected by special liners made from a semi-rigid plastic material and have the function of protecting part of the engine compartment and the inside of the wing from foreign bodies and dirt, of providing partial insulation against noise and of embellishing the parts of the housings that can be seen.

The engine compartment is protected at the bottom by a cover fastened to the chassis and to the bumper and thereis a series of covers at the top that can be easily removed for maintenance and repair operations.

The other elements (grilles, badges, moulding, etc.) are designed to enhance the style of the vehicle.

COMPOSITION

The absorbing buffers are fitted on all versions of the vehicle on the front crossmember connecting the struts.

The buffers are made from expanded polypropylene a material that maintains its properties of resistance to variations in temperature, ageging and any distortions.
