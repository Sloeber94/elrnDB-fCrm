1. Undo the rear connector (1a) and disconnect the rigid intermediate pipe from the connector to the left rear brake connector (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the front connector (1a) and disconnect the rigid intermediate pipe from the connector to the left rear brake connector (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the rigid intermediate pipe from the left rear brake connector (1a) from the underbody fastenings (1b) and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
