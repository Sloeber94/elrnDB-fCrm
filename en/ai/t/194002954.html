1060FA INJECTOR OPERATION CHECK

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Proceed as follows on the injector presumed to be faulty.

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Disconnect the wiring harness connector from engine control unit connector B

Turn the ignition key ON.

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is less than 0.3 V

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is greater than 500 kOhm

Move on to Step 6

Move on to Step 3

3

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the injector.

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is greater than 500 kOhm

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

4

Check that the resistance between Earth and:

  • Pin 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is greater than 500 kOhm

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

5

Replace the injector

The test can be considered over

6

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the injector.

Check that the resistance between:

  • Pin 16 and 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 2 and 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 1 and 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 17 and 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is greater than 500 kOhm

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring of the engine control unit

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

7

Check that the resistance between pin 1 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

and:

  • Pin 16 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 2 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 1 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 17 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is less than 5 Ohm

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

8

Check that the resistance between pin 2 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

and:

  • Pin 47 of the control unit for injector 1

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 31 of the control unit for injector 2

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 46 of the control unit for injector 3

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

  • Pin 33 of the control unit for injector 4

See M010 ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT

is less than 5 Ohm

Repair or, if necessary, replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the injector

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

9

Check that the resistance between pin 1 and 2 of the injector

See N070 INJECTOR

is between 0.3 and 0.7 Ohm

Replace the injector

10

Carry out a visual check on the condition of the injector and connector pipes to ensure it is okay

Replace the faulty component

11

Replace the engine management control unit
