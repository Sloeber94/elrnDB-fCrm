5580ED ELECTRONIC STEERING LOCK CHECK

Kind
test / check procedure
Applies to

Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fCrm/en/#/194003965

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods; only with equipment: MECHANICAL GEARBOX

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to Pin 1 and the negative earthed to Pin A4 of the steering lock control unit

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

the direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

Restore wiring continuity

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

2

Insert a key in the ignition and turn to STOP.

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin A6

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and negative earthed and read a battery voltage of approximately 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 4

Move on to Step 3

3

Check for wiring continuity between Pin 12 of the ignition device

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

and control unit Pin A6

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

Continue as instructed in the

See Test 5520AA CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY

4

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin B5

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and negative earthed.

Turn the ignition ON and check for a voltage of approximately 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 6

Continue as instructed in the

See Test 5520AA CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY

5

Check for wiring continuity between Pin 6 of the ignition device

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

and Pin B5 of the control unit [EL_M089] and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

Continue as instructed in the

See Test 5520AA CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY

6

Disconnect the wiring connector from the control unit

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Ohmmeter mode with one probe between Pin A5 of the control unit

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and the earth.

Press the clutch pedal and check for an earth signal.

Move on to Step 8

if no clutch signal is detected by the steering lock node, the car can still be started up by pressing the brake pedal

Move on to Step 7

7

Check the continuity of the wiring between Pin 4 of the clutch pedal switch

See I031 CLUTCH PEDAL SWITCH

and control unit Pin A5

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

If necessary, replace the clutch pedal switch

Op. 1060G42 SWITCH ON CLUTCH PEDAL - R.R. (JTD ENGINES)

The test can be considered over

Replace the steering lock node

Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

8

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin C5

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and negative earthed.

Press the clutch pedal.

Turn the ignition key to START.

Check for a battery voltage signal of 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 10

Move on to Step 9

9

Check for wiring continuity between Pin 5 of the ignition device

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

and control unit Pin C5

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

Continue as instructed in the

See Test 5520AA CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY

10

Turn the ignition key OFF.

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin C6

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and negative earthed.

Press the clutch pedal.

Turn the ignition key to START.

Check for a battery voltage signal of 12 Volts.

Move on to Step 12

Move on to Step 11

11

Check for wiring continuity between Pin 11 of the ignition device

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

and control unit Pin C6

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

Continue as instructed in the

See Test 5520AA CHECK ON IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY

12

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin B1

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and the negative earthed and read a battery voltage of approximately 12 Volts

Replace the steering lock node

Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

13

Turn the ignition key to the ON position.

Connect the tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin A1

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and the negative earthed and read a battery voltage of approximately 12 Volts

Replace the steering lock node

Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

14

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin C8

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and the negative earthed and read a battery voltage of approximately 12 Volts

Replace the steering lock node

Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R

15

Connect the diagnostic tester in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe to control unit Pin C4

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and negative earthed.

Press the brake pedal and the battery voltage reading should be 0 Volts.

Release the brake pedal and the voltage reading should be 12 volts.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 16

16

Check for wiring continuity between brake pedal switch Pin 4

See I030 BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH

and control unit Pin C4

See M089 STEERING LOCK CONTROL UNIT (NBS)

and repair if necessary

See E7065 ELECTRIC STEERING LOCK

The test can be considered over

Check the power supply is correct to the brake pedal switch and restore if necessary.

Move on to Step 17

17

Check the diagnostic instrument in parameter mode to ensure the brake pedal switch is operating correctly.

Replace the switch

Op. 1060G38 BRAKE PEDAL SWITCH - R.R

18

Replace the steering lock node

Op. 5580E17 STEERING LOCK - R.R
