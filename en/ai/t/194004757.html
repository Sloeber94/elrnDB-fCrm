CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive terminal on Pin 1 and the negative earthed to Pin 3 of the alarm siren

See P090 ALARM SIREN

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

Restore the connections between the Body Computer node, siren and earth

See E7015 ALARM

2

Disconnect the siren connectora Body Computer connector B and check the continuity of the wiring between Pin 2 of the siren connector and Pin 25 of Body Computer connector B

See P090 ALARM SIREN

See M001 BODY COMPUTER

Restore wiring continuity

See E7015 ALARM

3

Check the insulation of the earth line

See E7015 ALARM

Restore wiring insulation

4

Replace the siren

- Siren pinout

2. Serial line
