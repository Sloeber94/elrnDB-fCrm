J004 BACK WINDOW WIPER NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, when the lever on the right of the steering wheel is operated, the rearscreen wipers do not work properly, but the rearscreen washer does work

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the panel off, check that the pin is correctly fastened to the arm carrier by lifting the wiper blades up.

Move on to Step 1

The arm is not correctly secured (nut defective or not correctly tightened).

Replace and/or tighten the nut to torque.

1

REARSCREEN WIPER FUSE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the rearscreen wiper fuse

Move on to Step 2

The rearscreen wiper fuse is broken.

Replace the rearscreen wiper fuse (and find out what could have caused it to blow).

2

VEHICLE WIRING CONNECTIONS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Following the procedure,

Op. 7015A04 TAILGATE INTERIOR PANEL - R+R

remove the protective panel on the rear tailgate and check the condition of the vehicle wiring connector and make sure it is correctly fitted in the rearscreen wiper socket.

Move on to Step 3

1.Connector damaged.

1. Replace the connector.

2.Connector not correctly inserted.

2. Restore correct connection.

3

CHECK ON TENSION AT WIRING TERMINALS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Disconnect the connector from the rearscreen wiper and access the panel and select the various functions using the steering column switch unit checking the voltage at the wiring termiansl (correct voltage is the battery voltage 13.5 V).

Move on to Step 4

1. The voltage measured is not correct (the wiring is broken)

1. Restore the correct connections or replace the steering column switch unit.

2. The voltage measured is the same as that of the battery (rearscreen wiper defective)

2. Move on to Step 5.

3. Stalk unit not working properly.

3. Move on to Step 7.

4

REARSCREEN WIPER MOTOR POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the following, in order:

  • motor earth connection;
  • motor connection with steering column switch unit

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

Move on to Step 5

Electrical connection interrupted.

Repair the connections

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

5

REARSCREEN WIPER MOTOR CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the rearscreen wiper motor is working properly.

Move on to Step 6

Rearscreen wiper motor faulty.

Replace the rearscreen wiper motor

Op. 5050C42 REAR WIPER MOTOR - R+R

6

SCREEN WIPER CONTROL UNIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the electronic control device is working properly and, in particular, that the pins are not oxidized or distorted.

Move on to Step 7

Screen wiper control unit faulty.

Replace the screen wiper control unit

7

CHECK ON STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the stalk unit is working properly: in particular the rearscreen wiper switch

See descriptions 5550 WARNING AND MANOEUVRING INDICATORS

End of diagnosis

Stalk unit not working properly.

Replace the steering column switch unit

Op. 5550A10 STALK UNIT ASSEMBLY - R+R
