Removing

- Place the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder in its housing and connect it to the master cylinder (press fit without a band).

Make sure that the gasket is present and correctly positioned.Do not use mineral grease.

- Connect the flexible pipe from the clutch reservoir to the master cylinder, reservoir side and tighten the band.

- Place the A.B.S. electronic and hydraulic control unit in its housing, complete with mounting brackets.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the A.B.S. electronic and hydraulic control unit mounting brackets to the recommended torque.
