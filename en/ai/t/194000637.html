Refitting

1. Undo the two fixing bolts (1a) and remove the motor (1b) from the air conditioning casing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the control motor for the air conditioning flap is not damaged.

- Place the flap control motor in position.

- Tighten the two bolts fixing the flap control motor to the air conditioning casing and secure it.

Carry out the actuator vane position self-learning procedure using the Examiner.

- Check that the air conditioning is working properly.
