Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst the steering is operated there is a noise when it reaches the end of travel position.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir.

Conduct a test by steering fully to one side and then releasing the steering wheel.

Move on to Step 1

1. Fluid level too low.

1. Restore the correct level

2. The noise increases towards the end of travel position and with the engine speed (roar)

2. Move on to Step 1

3. The noise remains constant (hissing whilst steering, puffing on release)

3. No intervention is nececssary: the noise is due to the by-pass valve that discharges the excess pressure

1

CHECK ON POWER ASSISTED STEERING FLUID QUALITY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Visually inspect the power assisted steering fluid in the reservoir.

Make sure that there is no emulsified fluid (fluid that is lighter in colour with bubbles)

Move on to Step 2

Emulsified fluid.

Remove any air infiltrations from the power steering system pipes or replace the power steering reservoir

Op. 4130B25 POWER STEERING PUMP ASSEMBLY R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR CONDITIONER

2

POWER ASSISTED STEERING PIPES CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for constrictions in the flexible and rigid system pipes.

Move on to Step 3

Pipes faulty.

Replace the damaged parts, replacing all the seals

Op. 4130B POWER STEERING HYDRAULIC SYTEM

3

CHECK CORRECT OPERATION OF MPU (Motor Pump Unit)

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the MPU is working properly

See Test 4110DC ELECTRIC PUMP CHECK

End of diagnosis

MPU malfunction

Replace MPU

Op. 4130B25 POWER STEERING PUMP ASSEMBLY R R FOR VERSIONS WITH AIR CONDITIONER
