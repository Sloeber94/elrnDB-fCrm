Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

The flow of air sent by the fan in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

1

AIR DUCTS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the air ducts are intact and the vents are not blocked and are correctly positioned in the opening.

Move on to Step 2

1.Air ducts or vents obstructed

1.Restore the operation of the air ducts and the vents removing any material causing an obstruction.

2.The flow of air does not correspond to the distribution selected

2.Move on to Step 5

2

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF MAXIMUM SPEED RELAY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose, disconnect visually inspecting for any damage at the connector contacts. Reconnect the connection to the resistor. Check the operation of the fan at the maximum speed: if it only works at the maximum speed, the relay is broken. Check whether the resistor is broken by checking the electrical continuity in accordance with the test.

See Test 5040CF SEQUENTIAL PROCEDURE FOR CHECKING OPERATION OF AIR CONDITIONING FAN RESISTOR

Move on to Step 3

1.Electrical connections loose or damaged.

1. Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2.Relay broken.

2.Replace the ceramic resistor

Op. 5040C48 A/C FAN MOTOR RESISTOR - R+R

3.Resistance values faulty

3.Replace the ceramic resistor

Op. 5040C48 A/C FAN MOTOR RESISTOR - R+R

3

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF SPEED SELECTOR AT THE CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose, disconnect visually inspecting for any damage to the connector contacts. Reconnect the connection to the selector on the climate control system control unit. Check whether the selector at the control unit is broken by measuring the electrical continuity in accordance with the test

See Test 5040DF SEQUENTIAL PROCEDURE FOR CHECKING OPERATION OF AIR CONDITIONING FAN

Move on to Step 4

1.Electrical connections loose or damaged.

1. Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2.Fan not working.

2.Replace the speed selector knob

Op. 5040D23 KNOB (ONE) FOR ANY OF THE AIR CONDITIONING CONTROLS - R.R.

3.Power supply to the relays not correct.

3.Replace the climate control system control unit

Op. 5040D17 CONTROL UNIT WITH MANUAL AIR CONDITIONER KNOBS AND BUTTONS R R

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: AUTOMATIC AIR CONDITIONING

The flow of air sent by the fan in the passenger compartment using the manual speed control is not sufficient; it does not correspond to the speed set.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: AUTOMATIC AIR CONDITIONING

1

CHECK ON OPERATION OF FAN BY CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine started, set the climate control system control unit to the AUTO position, adjust the temperature to 22 °C, place the recirculation at the outside air intake and the distribution to the front position. Adjust the fan button (+ side) gradually increasing the speed set and then decrease the speed using the button (- side), thereby checking the effect of the air flow rate variation.

End of diagnosis

There is no variation in the air flow rate.

Move on to Step 2

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the diagnostic equipment and check:

  • that there are no errors at the passenger compartment fan;
  • that there are no errors at the timing actuator;
  • that there are no errors linked to the fan (short circuit errors)

Move on to Step 3

1.Error at passenger compartment fan.

1.Move on to Step 5

2.Fan operating module error.

2.Move on to Step 5

3.Error at the distribution actuator.

3.Move on to Step 6

4.Short circuit error.

4.Move on to Step 4

3

CHECK ON AIR DUCTS AND POLLEN FILTER

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the air ducts are intact and the vents are not blocked and are correctly positioned in the opening. Check that the pollen filter is not obstucted.

Move on to Step 4

1.Air ducts or vents obstructed.

1.Restore the operation of the air ducts and the vents removing any material causing an obstruction.

2.Pollen filter obstructed.

2.Replace the pollen filter

Op. 5010B09 PURIFYING (POLLEN) FILTER FILTER ELEMENT - REPLACE

4

CHECK ON CLIMATE CONTROL NODE ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the engine switched off, remove the control from the dashboard support and disconnect the cables connector from the control unit visually inspecting for any damage at the connector contacts and reconnect it correctly for check for any false contacts.

Move on to Step 5

Electrical connections loose or damaged.

Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

5

CHECK ON ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS AND FAN OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose. Disconnect/reconnect the connection to the engine mounting. Disconnect the connector and connect it to a feeder. In these conditions the engine should turn at the maximum speed indicating that the operating module is not working

Move on to Step 6

1.Electrical connections loose

Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2.The fan does not work.

2.Replace the fan

Op. 1088E10 RADIATOR COOLING FAN - R + R

3.The operating module does not work.

3.Replace the fan operating module

Op. 1088E20 RESISTOR FOR RADIATOR COOLING FAN - R + R

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION OF DISTRIBUTION ACTUATOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the electrical wiring connections are loose. Disconnect visually inspecting for any damage at the connector contacts and reconnect the connection to the engine mounting. If it persists, before replacing the actuator, check its operation by manually activating the function that the actuator should carry out. With the engine running, switch on the fan at maximum speed. Place the flaps in the various distribution positions via the control/control unit (manually). Check the air flow in the passenger compartment with your hand at the vents.

End of diagnosis

1.Electrical connections loose or damaged.

Renew the electrical connections

See E6020 AIR CONDITIONING

2.There is no air flow into the passenger compartment from the vents.

2.Move on to Step 7

7

CHECK FOR IMPEDIMENTS TO THE ROTATION OF THE DISTIRUBTION ACTUATOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the actuator and check using the control unit control that the actuator turns checking for mechanical impediments to its rotation.

End of diagnosis

1.The actuator does not turn.

1.Replace the timing actuator

Op. 5040D63 AIR DISTRIBUTION FLAP CONTROL ACTUATORS- R.R. WITH AIR CONDITIONING CASING REMOVED

2.Kinematic components or flaps broken/stuck

2.Replace the components that is faulty.
