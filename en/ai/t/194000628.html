5040D18 AIR CONDITIONING CONTROL UNIT - R.R.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

Removing

Refitting

Several of the CAN nodes, including the air conditioning control unit node M070, are programmed with default settings that the customer will find when they acquire the vehicle. If the air conditioning control unit is being replaced, the Parts Dept. will send a pristine component: once fitted, the data memorized in the body computer must be transferred to it by carrying out the "PROXI ALIGNMENT" procedure using the Examiner.

Description

Connector

CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

See M070 CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Description

Connector

BATTERY

See A001 BATTERY

- Working from inside the vehicle, carry out the following operations:

Op. 5580P12 CONNECT PANEL REPLACE

Op. 7040A56 SWITCH UNIT IN DASHBOARD - R.R.

Op. 7040A85 R R HANDLE FOR FRONT SEAT FORE AND AFT ADJUSTMENT

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the air conditioning control unit (1b) and partly extract it from its housing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

1. Disconnect the electrical connections (1a) and (1b) and remove the air conditioning control unit from the vehicle.
