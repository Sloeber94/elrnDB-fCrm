Op. 1048A10 AIR FILTER ASSEMBLY - R.R.

Op. 7040D30 ENGINE BAY FRONT BEAM COVER - R R

1. Position a hydraulic jack beneath the engine cooling radiator.

2. Undo the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supports to the front suspension crossmember.

- Lower the engine cooling radiator slowly using the hydraulic jack.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

1. Remove the cold air intake for the air filter (1a) after having released the retaining clips (1b) from the upper radiator crossmember.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the cold air intake for the air filter in its housing and fasten the retaining clips to the upper radiator crossmember.

- Place the engine cooling radiator in its housing using the hydraulic jack.

- Tighten the bolts fixing the engine cooling radiator supports to the front suspension crossmember.

- Remove the hydraulic jack from underneath the engine coolant radiator.
