7210E18 R.R. FRONT WING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

The vehicle should be in the following condition before the operation: front headlamp removed, front bumper trim removed, side warning light removed, bonnet open.

Check that the components connected are not distorted by checking the bodyshell alignment measurements using suitable equipment (gauges, templates or jigs).

- Straighten the body as necessary.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the wing front fixing bolts.

2. Undo the wing lower fixing bolt.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the upper bolts fixing the wing.

2. Remove the wing from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Position the wing and screw in the upper fixing bolts without tightening.

- Align the wing checking the surrounding lines with the door and bonnet.

- Screw in the fixing bolts in the wheel arch and tighten.

- Tighten the upper fixing bolts.

When the work is completed, refit the components removed previously.
