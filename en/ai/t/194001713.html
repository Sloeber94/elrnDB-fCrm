Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: RIGHT HAND DRIVE

The alarm system is managed by the alarm control unit integral with Body Computer M001 supplied directly from the battery (+30) from the line of maxifuse CPL B099 - pin 18 of connector F and a line protected by fuse F39 - pin 17 of connector F.

The ignition-operated power supply (INT from the steering lock control unit) reaches Body Computer M001 at pin 9 of connector F.

Body Computer M001 receives door open input signals at pin 8 connector D (driver's side front door), pin 25 connectorE (passenger side front door), pin 16 connector D (left rear door) and pin 26 connector E (right rear door).

These signals turn on the timed lights.

See E2510 COURTESY LIGHTS

The bonnet is controlled by switch I010 that connects to pin 24 of connector B of Body Computer M001 while the boot is controlled by switch I011 that is connected to pin 24 of connector E of Body Computer M001.

This switch turns on the boot light by means of a different contact.

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

The siren P090 receives a supply from the body computer M001, from pin 34 connector B and is connected with the dedicated serial line at pin 25 of connector B of Body Computer M001.

The volumetric - perimeter and antilift sensors K062 are supplied by pin 39 of connector E from Body Computer M001 and are connected via a dedicated serial line to pin 7 of connector D of Body Computer M001.

Unit K062 is supplied by an ignition-operated line (INT) protected by fuse E49 of under-dashboard control unit B002.

Volumetric sensor unit K062 encloses the buttons for turning off the volumetric sensors and antilift sensor.

Deterrent led E40 , located on the driver side front door, is connected to pins 6 and 8 of connector C of driver side door control unit M066

The remote control receiver aerial in ignition device H003 is connected to the Body Computer via the B-CAN network.

See E3040 CENTRAL LOCKING
