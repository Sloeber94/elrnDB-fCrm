The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0641 (Sensor power supply 1).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a short circuit to positive (detail "beyond the upper limit") or to earth (detail "below the lower limit") in the circuit connected to Pin 45 or 11 of the junction unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Junction unit connections check

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the engine management control unit connections are correct by carrying out the test

See Test 1060GM CHECK ON ENGINE MANAGEMENT CONTROL UNIT SENSOR CONNECTIONS 1

End of diagnosis
