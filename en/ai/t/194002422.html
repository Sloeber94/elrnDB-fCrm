Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the handbrake is operated the rear wheels are not locked. (A handbrake that is working properly should, when fully engaged, prevent the "take off" of the vehicle from stationary)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CONTROL CABLES CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check:

  • that the handbrake control cables are correctly fastened;
  • the condition of the actual cables.

Move on to Step 2

1. Cable fastening slack.

1. Restore correct fastenings

2. Cables damaged or broken.

2. Replace the cables involved

Op. 3380A30 HANDBRAKE CABLE - R+R

3. Rod damaged or broken.

3. Replace the rods involved

Op. 3380A30 HANDBRAKE CABLE - R+R

2

REAR BRAKE CALIPER CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the rear brake caliper pistons

End of diagnosis

Pistons worn or seized.

Replace the rear brake caliper involved

Op. 3310B36 REAR BRAKE CALIPER (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R
