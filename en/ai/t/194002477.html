K043 DISPLAY DOES NOT SHOW AVERAGE CONSUMPTION ON TRIP MODULE

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the fuel consumption gauge in the display (one of the TRIP COMPUTER module displays) is not working.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

N.B: The display provides two types of fuel consumption, instantaneous or average. The consumption is processed by the instrument panel node on the basis of the information received by the body computer node (distance) and by the engine management node (litres consumed) made available to the instrument panel node via the B-CAN and C-CAN respectively.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If speed information (from the Brake Node) or instantaneous consumption information (from the Engine Management Node) is missing, the consumption information will be replaced by "----"

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
