Refitting

Removing

1. Use adhesive tape to fold the lip of the windscreen seal to free the working area.

2. Use an appropriate tool, taking care not to damage the windscreen seal lip, cut and level the sealant strip to leave a thickness between 0.25 and 1 mm in the roof housing compartment on the body.

The thickness of the sealant remaining in the front roof housing will act as a support for the new bonding.

Thoroughly clean the roof housing compartment on the body with compressed air and degrease thoroughly with heptane

When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the windscreen to allow the primer thinner to evaporate.
