Applies to: all engines; all production periods

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition key OFF and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

WARNINGS

Whenever the battery terminals are reconnected, proceed as follows:

- initialise front and rear windows;

- initialise sun roof;

- initialise sun roof blind;

- reset instrument panel time and date;

- enter safety code in radio (if present);

- enter safety code in navigation system (if present);
