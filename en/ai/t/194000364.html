1076B20 REAR EXHAUST SILENCER - R + R

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Loosen the collar securing the rear exhaust silencer to the front exhaust silencer.

2. Remove the rear exhaust silencer (2a) after releasing it from the flexible supports (2b) and the front exhaust silencer.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rear exhaust silencer in its housing and secure to the flexible supports.

- Tighten the collar securing the rear exhaust silencer to the front exhaust silencer.

- Remove the vehicle from the lift.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

Op. 1076B12 EXHAUST PIPE ASSEMBLY R R

1. Working at the bench, loosen collar (1a) and remove the rear exhaust silencer (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rear exhaust silencer in its housing and secure with the collars.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.
