Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle can be fitted with a homologated tow hook for caravans or trailers in general.

This device must meet current regulations and be installed in accordance with the instructions in the installation kit.

This installation kit includes the tow hook and also an electronic control unit for the exterior trailer lighting plus specific wiring for connecting the tow hook to the vehicle tail-lights and a specific connection on the vehicle (D073), located in the boot on the right side.

If a trailer is connected, the parking sensor function is automatically inhibited.

The trailer control unit is supplied by a circuit protected by a special fuse from the underfacia junction unit.
