Applies to: all engines; all production periods

After rain or washing, water infiltration into the front part of the passenger compartment is noticed.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ABSENCE OF WINDSCREEN INFILTRATIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for sealant on the windscreen

See Test 7035OA CHECK ON NO PENETRATION FROM FIXED WINDOWS (WINDSCREEN AND REARSCREEN)

Move on to Step 2

No sealant present.

Renew the sealant

Op. 7035A04 WINDSCREEN - ELIMINATE LEAKS

2

CHECK THE WINDSCREEN

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the windscreen

In particular, conduct a visual check:

  • on the window condition;
  • on the flatness of the contact surface;
  • the flatness of the screen printing.

Move on to Step 3

Windscreen faults.

Replace the windscreen

Op. 7035A18 WINDSCREEN GLASS - R.R INCLUDING CLEANING HOUSING

3

CHECK ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD DRAINAGE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the engine compartment bulkhead drainages.

In particular, check: for dirt, leaves etc.

Move on to Step 4

Dirt in the drains.

Clean the drains. If necessary, restore the correct fitting.

4

CHECK THE ENGINE COMPARTMENT BULKHEAD

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the engine compartment bulkhead.

In particular, check:

  • the plugs are present;
  • the plugs are correctly fitted;
  • the bulkhead coupling rubber fittings are installed correctly;
  • sealant properly applied;
  • correct installation.

End of diagnosis

Faults in the engine compartment bulkhead.

Restore the engine compartment bulkhead installation and fastening
