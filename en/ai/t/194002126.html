Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the engine oil filler plug.

2. Undo the fixing bolts (2a) and remove the sound proofing cover (2b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the sound proofing cover back in its housing and secure it using the bolts.

- Place the engine oil filler cap in its housing.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Refitting
