Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

With the engine idling and warm, with all users off and the accelerator pedal not operated, the target Diesel Pressure and the measured Diesel Pressure must be in a range between 200 and 500 bars

Move on to Step 3

2

When the accelerator pedal is pumped several times, the measured Diesel Pressure must be in a range between 200 and 1500 bar.

The test can be considered over

Move on to Step 3

3

Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the fuel pressure sensor.

See K083 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR

Turn the ignition key ON. Measure the voltage between Pin 3 of the sensor and Earth, checking that it is within a range between 4.8 and 5.2 V.

See K083 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR

Move on to Step 5

Move on to Step 4

4

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine control unit and the debimeter.

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

5

Measure the voltage between Pins 1 and 3 of the sensor. Check it is in a range between 4.8 and 5.2 V

See K083 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR

Move on to Step 4

6

Measure the voltage between Pin 2 of the sensor and Earth, ensuring it is in a range between 4.8 and 5.2 V.

See K083 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR

Move on to Step 4

7

Replace the pressure sensor and check the fault has disappeared

Op. 1060G28 FUEL PRESSURE SENSOR ON FUEL MANIFOLD TO INJECTORS - R.R

Repair the original pressure sensor and replace the engine management control unit.

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
