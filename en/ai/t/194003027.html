Refitting

The procedure for the intake side phase transformer solenoid is described below, carry out the same instructions for the exhaust side solenoid.

1. Disconnect the electrical connection for the phase transformer solenoid, inlet side.

Description

Connector

1

Inlet side phase transformer solenoid valve

See N092 PHASE SHIFTER SOLENOID VALVE ON INTAKE SIDE

2. Undo the bolt and remove the phase transformer solenoid, inlet side.

Applies to: engines: 1.8; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the inlet side phase transformer solenoid in its housing and tighten the bolt.

- Connect the electrical connection for the phase transformer solenoid, inlet side.
