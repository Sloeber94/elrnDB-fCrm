- If the tappet clearance is not within the recommended tolerance, remove the camshaft involved and replace the cups selecting the size according to the required clearance.

The tappet cups are graded according to size and therefore the list of Spare Parts should be consulted when selecting the new cup to be fitted. The code is on the cup crown.

Applies to: engines: 1.8; all production periods
