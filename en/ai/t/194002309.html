Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

Changing the manual gearbox oil is not a planned maintenance operation and is only carried out during removing-refitting operations that involve draining the gearbox oil.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 7055B54 UNDER ENGINE PROTECTION/GUARD - R.R.

- Unscrew the manual gearbox oil drain plug and allow the oil to drain out into a suitable container.

Do not let the oil disperse into the environment as indiscriminate dispersion of this product causes pollution.

- Fit an oil drain plug complete with new seal and tighten it to the recommended torque.
