Difficulties in manoeuvring, loss of position, seat lifting device "hard" during operation/unsoldering.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK ON OPERATION OF RATCHET

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check on the operation of the seat lifting ratchet(s) (loss of position, hardness during adjustment).

Move on to Step 2

1. Seat does not stay in the position set.

1. Replace the seat lifting ratchet

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R

2. Difficulties with height adjustment.

2. Replace the seat lifting ratchet

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R

2

CHECK ON THE MATCHING BETWEEN THE SEAT LIFTING CONNECTING RODS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the matching between the seat lifting connecting rods.

End of diagnosis

1. Presence of weld residues that prevent correct operation.

1. Remove the interference for the connecting rods, space them out and lubricate them and replace the frame, if necessary.

Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.

2. The connecting rods rotate without interference.

2. Replace the seat lifting ratchet

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R
