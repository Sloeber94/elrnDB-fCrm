Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

The vehicle is equipped with two reversing lights located in the tail-lights (mobile part on the boot).

The reversing lights are operated whenever reverse is engaged by means of the gear lever, whether the gearbox is manual or automatic (position R).

The reversing light circuit is supplied with power with the key inserted and turned ON (INT from steering lock control unit) and protected by a fuse in the underfacia junction unit.

The reversing light activation signal is managed by the Body Computer, which forwards this information:

- to the steering wheel stalk unit for automatic operation of the rear wiper

See E3021 REAR WINDOW WASH/WIPE

- to the electrochrome interior mirror and the driving attitude node for memorised adjustment of the passenger side door mirror

See E3032 DOOR MIRROR ADJUSTMENT

- to the parking sensor control unit

See E3090 PARKING SENSOR

- to Connect Nav +

See E4090 NAVIGATOR
