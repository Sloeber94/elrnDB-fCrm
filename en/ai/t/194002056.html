ANTI-ROLL BAR CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that there are no faults with the anti-roll bar See Test

4410CA Verifiche sulla barra stabilizzatrice

More specifically:

  • Anti-roll bar correctly positioned in relation to the centre-line of the vehicle
  • condition (crumbling) of centre flexible mountings in the contact area with the bar;
  • Front fastenings tightened to torque between bar and chassis
  • Fastenings tightened to torque between bar and shock absorber connecting rod.

After disconnecting the upper ball joint of the shock absorber connection rod, also check that the antiroll bar does not show signs of squeaking/creaking in the central flexible blocks upon manual movement when a symmetrical load is applied.

SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the three bolts securing the block to the bod (on the shock absorber domes) are tightened to torque.

Also check that the positioning and the fitting of the semi-corner assembly (spring-shock absorber assembly) is correct.

See Test 4410AE CHECK ON CORRECT POSITIONING OF SPRING/SHOCK ABSORBER ASSEMBLY

To check this alignment, remove the semicorner fastening to the body and fastening to the knuckle.

Check the condition of the lower spring ring and the rubber coating of the gaiter on the fastening above the spring.

Check that there are no signs of rubbing on the suspension spring at its supports.

Also check that the spring shows no signs of wear

If the spring shows signs of abrasion, its mechanical properties could be weakened as a result of oxidation.

End of diagnosis

1. Semi corner units incorrectly positioned or fitted.

1. Restore the correct fitting/positioning of the components

2. Signs of tearing of lower rubber ring.

2. Repalce the lower rubber ring and ensure it is correctly oriented
