Removing

Refitting

In the following procedure the removing-refitting of the right lower track control arm is described, carry out the same operations for the left lower track control arm.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

1. Undo the nut fixing the steering rod to the right front steering knuckle.

2. Disconnect the steering rod (2a) from the right front steering knuckle using the tool (2b).

Do not use expansion tools for disconnecting the track control arm from the steering knuckle.

1. Undo the bolts fixing the lower track control arm to the front suspension frame.

Removing

- Place the front suspension track control arm in its housing and secure it to the front suspension frame without tightening the bolts to torque.

1. Connect the front suspension lower track control arm (1a) to the steering knuckle and secure it tightening a new bolt (1b) to the recommended torque.

Fit the bolt from the front towards the rear of the vehicle.
