Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When driving with the roof closed, a noise can be heard from the sunroof area

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check whether the noise only occurs with the roof closed.

Move on to Step 1

1. The noise can be heard with the roof open

1. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table S075 SUN ROOF NOISY / ROOF OPEN

2. The noise can be heard when the roof is opened in the swivel setting.

2. Proceed as instructed in the diagnosis

See table S077 SUN ROOF NOISY / WITH HINGED OPENING SUN ROOF

1

SUN ROOF FASTENING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fastening:

  • of the roof glass to the slides;
  • of the roof mechanism to the body

Move on to Step 2

Faulty fastenings.

Restore the fastening of the entire mechanism and the roof

Op. 7030B12 SLIDING ROOF (ASSEMBLY) - R+R

2

CHECK SUNROOF CONTROL DEVICES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the glass

Op. 7030B18 SLIDING ROOF GLASS PANEL - R+R

and check the operation of the sunroof operating devices:

  • ensure the felt pads are present on the central beam;
  • ensure the interior trim drive springs and present and correctly fastened;
  • ensure the teflon mount is present between the slides and rails;
  • ensure the felt pads are present between the spoiler and the front channel.

End of diagnosis

1. Faulty fastenings.

1. Restore all the components and the fastening of the entire mechanism

Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF

2. Faults in the sunroof operating devices.

2. Replace the affected parts

Op. 7030B SLIDING ROOF
