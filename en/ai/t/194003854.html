O003 FRONT SEAT ADJUSTMENTS (ALL) NOT WORKING

Path

Kind
fault diagnosis table (symptom -> numbered test steps)
Applies to

Interactive page
https://sloeber94.github.io/elrnDB-fCrm/en/#/194003854

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: ELECTRIC SEATS

All electric front seat functions (movement/adjustment) fail to work.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: ELECTRIC SEATS

1

CHECK ON CONDITION OF CIRCUIT PROTECTIVE FUSE

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the circuit protective fuse see wiring diagram

See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS

Move on to Step 2

Protective fuse blown

Replace the blown fuse

2

CHECK ON SWITCH STATUS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment:

Driver's/passenger seat control unit.

Check the value of the following parameter:

Sliding/seat lifting/backrest switch and check that it indicates released when the switch is released and indicates pressed when the switch is pressed.

Move on to Step 6

There is no change in the status of the switch.

Move on to Step 3

3

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT SLIDING/SEAT LIFTING/BACKREST SWITCH

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control switch.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/switch.

Move on to Step 4

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat mounting.

1.Fit connector correctly on switch.

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/switch.

4

CHECK ON WIRING CONNECTIONS AT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection for the wiring at the electric seat control unit.

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/control unit.

Move on to Step 5

1.Switch wiring connector detached or badly positioned on seat control unit.

1.Fit connector correctly on control unit

2. Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/control unit.

5

CHECK ON OPERATION OF SLIDING/SEAT LIFTING/BACKREST SWITCH

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the sliding/seat lifting/backrest switch is working properly

Move on to Step 11

Switch not working correctly.

Replace the switch

Op. 7045J50 DRIVER'S SIDE FRONT SEAT POSITION MEMORISATION SYSTEM ECU - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

6

CHECKS USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the diagnostic equipment connected, check:

that the sliding/seat lifting/backrest actuator is working properly through active diagnosis

Move on to Step 11

Incorrect operation of sliding/seat lifting/backrest actuator

Move on to Step 7

7

CHECK ON CONNECTION AT SLIDING/SEAT LIFTING/BACKREST MOTOR

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the connection is correctly attached to the motor

Also check for any oxidation/impurities at the connector/motor.

Move on to Step 8

1.Connectors at motor detached or badly positioned.

1.Fit connector correctly on motor

2.Presence of oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

2.Eliminate oxidation/impurities at connector/motor.

8

SEAT WIRING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection between the seat connection and the floor connection

Move on to Step 9

Seat connectors detached from vehicle floor connectors

Restore the correct connections between the seat and the floor connections

9

CHECK ON ELECTRICAL SEAT WIRING

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the electric seat wiring

Move on to Step 10

Presence of cuts/tears to seat wiring.

Replace cut/broken wiring see wiring diagram

See E3076 ELECTRICALLY-ADJUSTABLE SEATS

10

CHECK ON SLIDING/SEAT LIFTING/BACKREST ADJUSTMENT MOTORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the sliding/seat lifting/backrest movement motors are working properly.

Move on to Step 11

1.Sliding adjustment motor not working properly.

1.Replace sliding adjustment motor

Op. 7045J26 FRONT SEAT SLIDE MOTOR, L/H OR R/H - R+R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

2. Seat lifting adjustment motor not working properly.

2. Replace the seat lifting adjustment motor

Op. 7045J30 FRONT SEAT LIFTING MOTOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

3. Backrest adjustment motor not working properly.

Replace the backrest adjustment motor

Op. 7045J34 FRONT SEAT BACKREST ANGLE MOTOR, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R. FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL WIRING CHECK

11

DRIVER'S/PASSENGER SEAT CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the driver's/passenger seat control unit

Op. 7045J36 SEAT MOVEMENT ECU R R FOLLOWING ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem
