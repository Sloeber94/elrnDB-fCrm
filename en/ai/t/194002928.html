The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2149 (Group 2 injector control).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if the voltage mesured by the control unit at the energizing outlet module for hte injectors does not correspond to the nominal value.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods
