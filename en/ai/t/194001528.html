Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: Radio setup

The vehicle is equipped with a radio system comprising:

- wiring (speakers and radio);

- speakers in the mirror triangle (tweeters);

- speakers in the front doors (mid-woofers);

- speakers in the rear door (full-range);

- AM/FM aerial coaxial cable;

- aerial mounting and stem.

With the above preparation it is possible to fit a Linea Accessori or After Market radio.

The radio leads and speaker leads are grouped together by means of two connectors (both 8-way) located and secured in the rear part of the compartment (1 DIN dimensions) which should be removed when fitting the radio.

In this configuration there is no CAN, therefore the customer does not have a series of functions that are available on the radio as the initial system.
