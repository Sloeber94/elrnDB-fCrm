Removing

Refitting

- Open the rear tailgate.

- Fold over the tab for the load carrying compartment cover.

1. Working on both sides, undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the hooks (1b) from the moulding.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the tailgate housing seal for the section involved.

2. Undo the six bolts fixing the luggage/load carrying compartment moulding.

3. Using the special tool, release the inner retainers (3a) and remove the moulding (3b) for the luggage/load carrying compartment.

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the moulding for the luggage/load carrying compartment is not damaged.

- Place the moulding correctly in its housing by the fastening openings.

- Press the inner retainers for the moulding on the bodyshell.

- Position the hooks and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the rear tailgate seal in place. Fold over the load carrying compartment cover tab.
