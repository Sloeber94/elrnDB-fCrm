Whenever repairs are carried out on the rear suspension that require the front or rear wishbone geometry (toe in and camber) adjustment bolts to be loosened, or the front or rear track control arm, it is absolutely necessary to check the rear suspension angles

Op. 4450A40 REAR WHEEL GEOMETRY - CHECK USING OPTICAL EQUIPMENT, INCLUDES ADJUSTMENT IF NECESSARY

The following procedure describes left rear wishbone removal/refitting. Proceed in the same way for the right rear wishbone.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

Op. 4420A40 L/H OR R/H REAR SUSPENSION COIL SPRING (ONE) - R+R

1. Unscrew the bolts (1a) and (1b), then remove the rear suspension rear track control arm (1c).
