Applies to: all engines; all production periods

it should be remembered that xenon bulbs do not have a filament and cannot therefore be tested using an ohmmeter

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the master (left side) headlamp control unit.

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the slave (right side) headlamp control unit.

Move on to Step 2

1. Error B1010 - xenon lamp fails to come on detected

1. Move on to Step 4

2. Error B1050 - power supply wiring resistance detected

2. Move on to Step 3

3. Error B1001 - xenon lamp high tension operation detected

3. Replace the lamp control unit where the error was detected

Op. 5540B42 CONTROL UNIT OF ONE GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER R R

2

IGNTION-OPERATION POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the ignition key in the ON position, check that the voltage at the headlamps si correct by carrying out the

See Test 5540BJ IGNITION-OPERATED HEADLAMPS POWER SUPPLY CHECK

Move on to Step 3

3

DIPPED BEAM POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Turn the steering column switch unit lever to the dipped headlamps position and check that the voltage at the headlamps is correct, by carrying out the

See Test 5540BA CHECK ON DIPPED HEADLAMPS CIRCUIT

Move on to Step 4

4

BULB OPERATION CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the bulbs are working by tyring to replace them

Op. 5540B44 DIPPED BEAM FOR ONE GAS DISCHARGE LIGHTING UNIT ONE LH OR RH R R

End of diagnosis

1. Right headlamp bulb defective (after it is replaced it works correctly)

1. Restore the original bulb for the left headlamp (which is still off) and move on to Step 5

2. Left headlamp bulb defective (after it is replaced it works correctly)

2. Restore the original bulb for the right headlamp (which is still off) and move on to Step 5

5

HEADLAMP WIRING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the continuity of the wiring between the 14-way headlamp connector

Mater and Slave and control unit side connector, by carrying out the

See Test 5540BK CHECK ON CONTINUITY OF DIPPED HEADLAMP WIRING INSIDE HEADLAMP

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem
