Refitting

Removing

- Assemble the flexible gear selection and engagement rods with the mounting bracket and secure them using the retaining clips.

- Place the flexible gear selection and engagement rods in their housings introducing them from the engine compartment towards the passenger compartment.

Check that the rod support diaphragm is correctly housed in the engine compartment bulkhead and that the arrow stamped on it is pointing upwards.

- Connect the gear selection and engagement flexible rods to the gearbox.

Make sure that the gear lever is in neutral.

- Tighten the bolt fixing the gear selector-engagement rods mounting bracket.

- Secure the gear selection and engagement cable links to the gearbox control lever support and do up the clips.

- Connect the gear selector and engagement cables to the manual gearbox control lever.

Refitting

Removing

- Place the flexible gear selection and engagement rods in their housings introducing them from the engine compartment towards the passenger compartment.

Check that the rod support diaphragm is correctly housed in the engine compartment bulkhead and that the arrow stamped on it is pointing upwards.

- Connect the gear selector and engagement cables to the manual gearbox control lever.

Make sure that the gear lever is in neutral.

- Introduce the two retaining clips for the cables in the openings in the reaction bracket and secure them exerting manual pressure until they are properly attached.

- Connect the ends to the spherical ends of the gear selection and engagement levers.

- Remove the anti-release device and operate the lever to disarm the locking cursor for the moving selection terminal.
