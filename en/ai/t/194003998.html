Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

1. Undo the nuts securing the engine oil cooling radiator to the mounting bracket.

- Move the engine oil cooling radiator to the side without disconnecting the pipes.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1. Undo the upper bolts securing the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket to the bodyshell.

2. Undo the lower bolt securing the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket to the bodyshell.

This bolt also secures the front suspension frame to the bodyshell.

3. Remove the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the engine oil cooling radiator mounting in its housing.

- Tighten the lower bolt securing the engine oil cooling radiator mounting bracket to the bodyshell to the recommended torque.

This bolt also secures the front suspension frame to the bodyshell.
