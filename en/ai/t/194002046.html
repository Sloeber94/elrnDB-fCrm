S063 REAR DOORS CREAKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the vehicle moving, particularly over rough ground or during acceleration or braking, noise can be heard from the one of the rear doors.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK DOOR FASTENINGS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fastening:

  • of the door opening limiter;
  • of the hinges (upper and lower);
  • of the lock.

Move on to Step 2

1. Faults in limiter fastening

1. If necessary, lubricate the limiter and restore the correct fastening

Op. 7010A28 REAR SIDE DOOR OPENING LIMITER - R.R

2. Faults in hinge fastening.

2. If necessary, lubricate the hinge pin and restore the correct fastening

Op. 7010A24 REAR SIDE DOOR HINGES (BOLTED) - REPAIR OR REPLACE WITH DOOR REMOVED

3. Faults in lock fastening

3. Restore the correct fastening of the lock

Op. 7010N21 REAR SIDE DOOR LOCK MOTOR/LOCK ASSEMBLY, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

4. Faults in striker fastening.

4. Check that the striker fastening is correct

Op. 7010B14 REAR SIDE DOOR LOCK STRIKER - R.R

2

CHECK DOOR PANEL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Remove the door panel and check for foreign bodies inside the door

Op. 7010E10 REAR SIDE DOOR INNER PANEL - R.R. OR REPLACE

Move on to Step 3

Foreign bodies present.

Remove the foreign bodies.

3

CHECK DOOR INNER FASTENINGS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the fastening:

  • of the window mechanism;
  • of the moving window guides;
  • of the moving window fastening element;
  • of the door opening/closure linkage.

End of diagnosis

1. Window fastening faults.

1. Restore the correct fastening of the window

Op. 7010M26 ELECTRIC WINDOW OPENING MECHANISM FOR ONE REAR DOOR LH OR RH - R.R

2. Faults in the fastening of the guides and fastening element

2. Restore the correct fastening of the guides

Op. 7010D14 REAR SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.E. WITH DOOR PANEL (AND SUPPORT FRAME) REMOVED

3. Fastening element anomalies

3. Replace the fastening element

Op. 7010D18 REAR SIDE DOOR DESCENDING WINDOW GLASS CHANNEL (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

4. Faults in linkage fastening.

4. Restore the linkage fastening clips, see Assembly

Op. 7010C REAR SIDE DOOR LOCK CONTROLS
