Removing

Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the front section of the exhaust pipe (1b), catalytic converter side.

Fasten the disconnected part of the exhaust pipe so that it is not hanging loose. The front section of the exhaust pipe including the flexible pipe can be fastened to the bodyshell appropriately using, for example metal wire. Bends in the flexible pipe at angles of between 5° and 10°, starting from the planned fitting position, can damage the flexible pipe.

2. Remove the gasket.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a), (1b) and remove the bracket on the gearbox for the lower reaction rod (1c).

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Refit the lower reaction rod bracket (1a) in its seat on the gearbox and secure it by tightening the bolts (1b), (1c) to the recommended torque.
