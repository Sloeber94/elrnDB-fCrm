Refitting

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the panel is not damaged and check that the inner retaining clips are intact and correctly positioned.

- Place the panel correctly in position in its housing and press on the mounting points to fit the clips.

- Fit the securing buttons.

- Tighten the retaining bolts.

- Restore the covers.
