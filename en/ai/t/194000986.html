1. Release the blind (1a) from the drivers (1b) using a circular movement.

2. Using the special tool, release the end of travel pin (2a), undo the bolts (2b) and remove the brackets (2c) on both sides.

Removing the end of travel pins destroys them; use new replacement parts when refitting.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) on both sides, the five fixing bolts (1b) and remove the crossmember (1c) complete with blind.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and (1b) and release the support (1c) from the pipes.

2. Undo the four fixing bolts (2a) and remove the sliding guide (2b) releasing the bowden cable from the pipe.

Do not release the bowden cable for the sliding blind driver from the pipe.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the sun roof sliding guide is not damaged and that the pulley slides without impediment.

- Fit the bowden cable in the pipe, fit the driver in the guide, place it in position and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit the mounting on the pipes and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Place the centre crossmember in the vehicle fitting the blind in the guides, tighten the fixing bolts to the sun roof frame and to the sun roof guides.

- Fit the mounting brackets and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit new end of travel pins and attach the blind to the drivers.
