- Secure the handbrake lever self-adjustment system as follows.

- Move the handbrake lever to rest position.

1. Press the link spring to slacken tension in the cables.

2. Apply sufficient force to lock the self-adjustment system and engage the retaining clip to the teeth.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the handbrake control cable from the rear brake calipers.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the handbrake control cables from the intermediate fastenings.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove retaining ring (1a) and extract connection pin (1b).

2. Remove the handbrake control cable assembly by disengaging from the fastenings on the body.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Refit the handbrake control cable assemblies in their seats and secure to the body.

- Refit the connection pin in its seat and secure with a new retaining ring.

- Secure the handbrake control cables to the intermediate fastenings.

- Connect the handbrake control cable to the rear brake calipers.

- Pull the handbrake control lever by hand to the end of its travel until the retaining clip is released.

- Move the lever to rest position.

- Then move the handbrake control lever to the end of its travel during the next four operations (maximum possible travel) to fully stabilise the self-adjustment system.
