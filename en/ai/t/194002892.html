The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0651 (Sensor power supply 2).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a short circuit to positive (detail "beyond the upper limit") or a short circuit to earth or a brek (detail "below the lower limit") in the circuit connected to Pin 46, 13 or 28 of the junction unit.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods
