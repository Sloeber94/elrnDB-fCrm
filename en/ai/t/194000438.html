Op. 5520C20 HEATER PLUGS CONTROL UNIT - R.R

1. Lift up the retaining clip (1a) and disconnect the flexible pipe from the master cylinder to the hydraulic clutch actuator (1b), vibration damper side.

2. Lift the retaining clip (2a) and disconnect the clutch hydraulic circuit vibration damper (2b), clutch master cylinder side and remove it.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the clutch hydraulic circuit vibration damper back in its housing, connect it to the master cylinder and secure it using the retaining clip.

- Connect the flexible pipe from the master cylinder to the hydraulic clutch actuator, vibration damper side and secure it using the retaining clip.
