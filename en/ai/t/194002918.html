The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2085 (temp. sensor at particle filter inlet).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if voltage at Pin 32 of the electronic control module is greater than 2.23 V (short circuit to positive) (detail "short circuit to +Vbatt or open circuit") or less than 0.66 V (short circuit to earth or break in the circuit) (detail "short circuit to earth").

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods
