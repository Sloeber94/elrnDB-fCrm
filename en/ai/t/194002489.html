K071 ELECTRIC STEERING WARNING LIGHT OFF / (DOES NOT CARRY OUT INITIAL TEST)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the ignition is turned ON, the electric steering fault warning light does not come on in any case.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner diagnostic equipment and check, in the Instrument Panel Node ambient, that there are no errors and that the parameters are present.

Move on to Step 7

1. Impossible to connect to the NQS control unit.

1. Move on to Step 2.

2. Presence of errors

2. Move on to Step 5.

2

ELECTRICAL POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the electricity supply to the Instrument Panel Node is correct.

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 3

Fault in electrical supply positive and/or earth.

Replace defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

3

CHECK ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the Instrument Panel Node electrical connections, particularl that each pin is correctly connected and inserted.

Move on to Step 4

NQS electrical connections not operational.

Renew the damaged electrical connections

4

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Use an Examiner to check the possibility of connection to the Instrument Panel Node

Move on to Step 5

Impossible to connect with the control unit

Replace the Instrument Panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

5

CHECK ERRORS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for the absence of the specific error with the Examiner in Instrument Panel Mode.

Move on to Step 7

Internal NQS control unit error detected

Move on to Step 6.

6

ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

With the Examiner in the Instrument Panel Node mode check that the function in question is correctly activated in Active Diagnosis.

Move on to Step 7

The component in question is not activated.

Replace the Instrument Panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

7

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis

Fault persists

Continue as described by the diagnostic equipment
