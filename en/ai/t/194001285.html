Applies to: all engines; all production periods

“MINI F.L.Ore.N.C.E”. architecture provides a serial interface connection between various vehicle components. This line is known as the A-BUS SERIAL LINE.

The A-bus serial line manages alarm, SOS, windscreen wiper, lighting and tyre pressure measurement functions.

The cross-section of the cables connected to the A-bus line is 0.35 mm².
