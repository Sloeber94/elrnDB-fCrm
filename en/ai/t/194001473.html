Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: RIGHT HAND DRIVE

Both door control units on the driver side M066 and passenger side M067 are supplied directly from the battery via the control logic described above (and controlled by the Body Computer M001 pin 14 of connector F) via service relay T13, located in the under-dashboard control unit B002, via two lines protected by fuses F47 and F48. The supply reaches pin 8 of connector D in both cases.

Control units M066 and M067 are connected to one another and to Body Computer M001 via the CAN line, at pins 15 and 16 of connector B.

Unit H044 contains the controls for the driver and passenger side window controls: dedicated analogue signals are sent to pins 31 and 33 of connector A of M66 (pin 32 supplies a reference earth)..

Control unit Mo66 operates the motor N060 (driver's side) directly - pins 9 and 10 of connector D - and via control unit M067 - pins 9 and 10 of connector D - motor N061 (passenger side).

Similarly, the passenger side front door control H050 sends an analogue signal to control unit M067 pin 32 (pin 33 supplies a reference earth), which drives motor N061 .

Pins 2 and 5 of connector C of M066 and M067 receive signals from the antipinch sensors in window motors N060 and N061.

The two control units are connected to the instrument panel E050 via the CAN line to manage the general fault warning light if there is a failure in the anti-pinch safety sensors.

Body Computer M001 - pins 24 and 25 of connector H - is connected via the CAN line to ignition H003 - pins 1 and 2 - for centralised window closure.
