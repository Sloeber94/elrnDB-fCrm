The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0403 (EGR valve), with detail "open circuit"

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is a break in the circuit connected to Pin 60 of the junction unit. As a result the maximum power is reduced and the exhaust gas recirculation valve is switched off.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods
