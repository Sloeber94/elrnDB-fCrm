1. Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

2. Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

3. Loosen the bleed valve on the brake caliper and wait until the brake-clutch reservoir and pipes drains.

Refitting

Removing

- Clean the sealing surfaces of the brake master cylinder and the brake-clutch fluid reservoir.

Do not use mineral oils or grease.

- Place the new seals in their housings on the brake master cylinder checking that they are correctly positioned.

- Place the brake-clutch fluid reservoir in its housing complete with minimum fluid level warning light switch and secure it using the bolt.

- Connect the flexible pipe from the clutch fluid reservoir to the electro-hydraulic clutch assembly, reservoir side and tighten the band.

- Connect the electrical connection to the brake fluid minimum level warning light switch.

- Connect the negative battery terminal.
