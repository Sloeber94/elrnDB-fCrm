0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the engine oil level is correct

Op. 0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

Check that the air intake/vacuum circuit is working properly by carrying out the

See Test 1048OA AIR INTAKE/VACUUM CIRCUIT CHECK

Check the condition of the fuel supply circuit and make sure that it is working properly carrying out the

See Test 1040OA FUEL SUPPLY CIRCUIT CHECK

Also check

. wiring on engine correctly fastened to earth;

. battery correctly fastened to earth;

. positive battery pole and all leads connected to it correctly fastened

Move on to Step 1

Oil level incorrect

Restore the correct engine oil level

Op. 0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

Supply fastenings slack or oxidized

Restore correct fastenings

See E1010 POWER SUPPLY

Earth fastenings slack or oxidized

Restore correct fastenings

See E1010 POWER SUPPLY

AMBIENT PARAMETERS CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Select the following control unit at the diagnostic equipment: EDC16C39 Diesel electronic injection control unit.

Check the value of the following parameters:

. Battery voltage

See Test 5530BB BATTERY VOLTAGE CHECK

. Engine coolant temperature

See Test 1060OE CHECK ON OPERATION OF COOLANT TEMPERATURE SENSOR

. Diesel temperature

See Test 1060EA DIESEL FUEL TEMPERATURE CHECK

. Target Diesel pressure

. Measured Diesel pressure

See Test 1060GH DIESEL FUEL PRESSURE CHECK

. Engine rpm

See Test 1060GI ENGINE SPEED CHECK

Move on to Step 2

ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the fuses F1 (the last one at the bottom of the vertical row) and F20 (the first one on the right of the lower row) have not blown.

The check on the following system is only necessary if the outside temperature is below 0 °C.

Move on to Step 5

Fuse F1 has blown

Restore the connections or, if necessary, replace the junction unit in the engine compartment

Op. 5505A13 ADDITIONAL JUNCTION UNIT IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT - R.R.

Fuse F20 has blown

Move on to Step 4

Operation

LOW RETURN PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1060OA CHECK ON RETURN LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

that the pressure in the return pipe to the tank is less than 1 bar.

Move on to Step 9

Pressure above the maximum limit: return pipe to the tank obstructed

Repair the pipe, if necessary, replace it

9

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

that the diesel pressure between the filter and the pressure pump is between 2 and 3 bar.

Move on to Step 15

Pressure below the minimum limit or above the maximum limit

Move on to Step 10

10

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Constrict the return pipe downstream of the valve and carry out the test once again

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

with the pipe constricted checking that the filter outlet pressure remains below 2 bar

Move on to Step 11

Pressure rises to 5 bar: pressure regulator valve on filter mounting incorrectly adjusted

Replace the valve

Op. 1044B10 FUEL FILTER ASSEMBLY - R + R OR REPLACE

11

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the difference in pressure upstream and downstream of the filter is less than 1 bar

Move on to Step 12

Difference in pressure more than 1 bar

Replace the fuel filter cartridge

Op. 1044B20 FUEL FILTER FILTERING ELEMENT - REPLACE

12

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the supply pump pressure at the filter inlet is more than 2.2 bar

Move on to Step 15

Pressure below 2.2 bar

Move on to Step 13

13

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Measure the difference in pressure between the electric pump outlet and the filter inlet and check that it is less than 0.3 bar

Move on to Step 14

Difference in pressure more than 0.3 bar

Replace the pipe

14

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check as described in the

See Test 1040AA SUPPLY VOLTAGE AND ABSORPTION CURRENT CHECKS

that the pump supply voltage is equal to 12V and that the current uptake is between 2 and 10 A

Move on to Step 15

Voltage lower than 12 V

Renew electrical wiring

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

Current greater than 10 A: pump defective

Replace the fuel pump

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

Current less than 2 A: pump obstructed

Replace the fuel pump

Op. 1040A70 SUBMERGED PUMP ASSEMBLY COMPLETE WITH LEVEL GAUGE CONTROL - R + R

and clean the fuel tank
