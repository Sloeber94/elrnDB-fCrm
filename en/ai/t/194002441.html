STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check power supply (+15)

  • Connect using the Examiner in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe at pin 1 and the negative probe of the 5-way connector for the connector plate for the MPU earthed:
  • Turn the key to the on position and read the battery voltage of around 12 volt

Check the continuity of the 10 A fuse F51 in the dashboard junction unit and if broken replace it checking out the cause.

Check the continuity of the wiring between the fuse and the MPU

2

Check earth connection

Check the continuity of the connection between pin 2 of the two-way supply connector for the MPU connector plate and the earth

Restore the continuity of the earth connection

3

Check power supply (+30)

Connect using the Examiner in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe at pin 1 and the negative probe of the two-way connector for the connector plate for the MPU earthed.

Check the continuity of the 80 A fuse F73 in the battery junction unit and if broken replace it checking out the cause.
