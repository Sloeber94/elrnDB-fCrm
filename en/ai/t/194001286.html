Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Body Computer M001 is connected to the A-Bus serial line via:

- pin 25 of connector B, for vehicle components on the facia and front wiring;

- pin 7 of connector D, for vehicle components on the rear wiring.

The components involved are:

- steering wheel stalk unit H005, pin 2 of connector A;

- tyre pressure control unit M047, pin 18 of connector B;

- alarm siren P090, pin 2;

- the volumetric sensor unit for alarm K062, pin 4;

- the dusk sensor built into the electrochromic interior mirror unit P065, pin 5.

The connections are made through short-circuiting junctions D097 located in the various engine cables.
