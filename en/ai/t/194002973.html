Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • With an Examiner connected in Voltmeter mode with the postive probe at pin 1 and the negative earthed at pin 7 and with the ignition key on, check for a voltage reading of 12 Volts
  • When an Examiner is connected in Voltmeter mode with the positive probe at pin 2 and the negative earthed, check for a voltage reading of 12 Volts
