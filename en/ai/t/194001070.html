Removing

Refitting

- Open the rear tailgate.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Disconnect the negative and positive battery terminals.

Op. 7040B24 LOAD COMPARTMENT TOP TRIM (ONE) - R+R

1. Remove the cover located in the upper part of the trim, unscrew bolts (1a) and (1b), then remove the rear pillar trim (1c) from the vehicle.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the rear pillar cover is not damaged.

- Position the rear pillar trim in the housing and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Reposition the cover located in the upper part of the trim.
