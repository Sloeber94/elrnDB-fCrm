- Undo the bolts fixing the brake servo to the pedal assembly.

- Extract the pin connecting the brake servo to the brake pedal.

- Disconnect the master cylinder push rod from the clutch pedal.

- Remove the brake pedal and clutch pedal switches.

- Undo the nuts and remove the pedal assembly.

- Undo the bolts and separate the support from the pedal assembly.

The brake and clutch pedals cannot be dismantled from the pedal assembly.

Applies to: engines: 2.2, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods
