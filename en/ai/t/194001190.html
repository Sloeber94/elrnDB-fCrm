Before removing and refitting the Air Bag module the following preliminary operations must be carried out.Fit and remove in the order as described.The fitting and the dismantling of the buckle must be carried out with the battery disconnected and the panel off (Teg Reader not inserted).Do not reconnect the battery until the fitting is correctly completed.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

- Disconnect the negative and positive battery terminals and insulate them.

- Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.
