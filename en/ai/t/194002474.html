K034 LIGHTS CHECK WARNING LIGHT OFF (INITIAL TEST NOT CARRIED OUT)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

With the ignition ON, the Body Computer does not display any irregular operation in one or more types of lights:

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

  • side lights
  • brake lights
  • rear fog lamps
  • direction indicators
  • no. plate lights

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

These lights, on the other hand, are not working, but the Body Computer does not light up the relevant "Lights failure" warning light

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Using the Examiner diagnostic equipment check, in the Instrument Panel Node ambient, that there are no errors and that the parameters are present.

Move on to Step 5

1. Impossible to connect with the Instrument Panel control unit

1. Move on to Step 3.

2. Presence of errors

2. Continue following the information for the diganostic equipment

2

ELECTRICAL POWER SUPPLY CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the electrical power supply for the "Instrument Panel Node" is correct see wiring diagram

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 3

Positive and/or earth electrical power supply faulty

Replace defective component (in the case of a fuse, find out what caused it to blow).

3

ELECTRICAL CONNECTIONS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the electrical connections between the Body Computer and the Instrument Panel, in particular that every single pin is correctly connected

Move on to Step 4

Electrical connections not working

Restore the damaged electrical connections see wiring diagram

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

4

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Using the Examiner, check the possibility of connecting with the Instrument Panel Node

Move on to Step 5

Impossible to connect with the Instrument Panel

Replace the instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

5

INSTRUMENT PANEL ACTIVE DIAGNOSIS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check the operation of the lights check warning light in the instrument panel through active diagnosis

Move on to Step 6

The lights failure warning light does not come on

Replace the instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

6

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
