Removing

Refitting

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

- Open the bonnet.

Op. 5530B40 SUPPLY BOX ON BATTERY (LINK BATTERY AND FUSE BOX) - R R

1. Remove the protective battery cover, lifting it up and releasing it from the two retaining pins underneath.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Open the lever (1a) and detach the negative battery terminal (1b).

BATTERY

See A001 BATTERY

Take care whilst opening the lever on account of the release device.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Remove the protective cover for the supply casing.

1. Undo the fixing bolts (1a) and remove the battery retaining bracket (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Unscrew the power supply casing anchorage bolts

2. Undo the nut fixing the positive terminal.

3. Disconnect the power supply casing and move out of the working area.

Description

Connector

3

MAXI FUSE BOX ON BATTERY

See B099 MAXI FUSE BOX ON BATTERY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the battery from the engine compartment.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the battery is not damaged and check it is working properly.

- Place the battery on the drip tray.

- Position the battery retaining bracket and tighten the two fixing bolts.

- Position the power supply casing in its seat, tighten the anchorage bolts and the positive terminal nut.

- Fit the casing protective cover.
