Refitting

Removing

- Check that the wiring is not damaged and that the individual conducts are correctly fastened to the connections.

- If necessary, carry out continuity tests.

- Secure the parking sensor wiring in the retaining collars inside the rear bumper.

- Connect the electrical connections to the parking sensors.
