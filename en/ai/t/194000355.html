Lambda sensor on catalyzer

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the front Lambda sensor from the catalytic converter and remove it.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) and remove the heat shield (2b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and disconnect the front section of the exhaust pipe (1b), exhaust manifold side with the catalytic converter.

Fasten the disconnected part of the exhaust pipe so that it is not hanging loose. The front section of the exhaust pipe including the flexible pipe can be fastened to the bodyshell appropriately using, for example metal wire.Bends in the flexible pipe at angles of between 5° and 10°, starting from the planned fitting position, can damage the flexible pipe.

2. Remove the gasket.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the nuts (1a) and remove the exhaust manifold and catalytic converter (1b).

- Remove the gasket.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods
