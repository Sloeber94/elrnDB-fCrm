With the engine running and the vehicle stationary, the steering wheel oscillates and vibrates.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the tyre pressure.

First of all, check the engine idle speed

Move on to Step 1

1. Engine idle irregular

1. Proceed as instructed in the

See table D010 ENGINE IDLE IRREGULAR

2. Tyre inflation pressure not correct

2. Make sure tyres are inflated to the correct pressure.

1

ENGINE FLEXIBLE MOUNTINGS CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the engine frame, supports and connecting rod connecting the engine to the engine frame.

Move on to Step 2

1. Engine flexible mountings faulty.

1. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.

Op. 1008A POWER UNIT FASTENINGS

2. Connecting rod faulty.

2. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.

Op. 1008C POWER UNIT REACTION ELEMENTS

3. Engine frame faulty.

3. Adjust the fastenings: loosen the bolts, centre them in their housings and tighten them to the recommended torque.

2

STEERING COLUMN CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the steering column support and that it is working properly.

In particular, check:

  • that there is no clearance between the support bushes and steering column;
  • correct tightening of steering column joints.

Move on to Step 3

1. Bushes worn or damaged.

1. Restore the efficiency of the steering column. If necessary, replace the bearings involved.

2. Joint spider clearance too large.

2. Tighten the bolts or replace the steering column

Op. 4110A30 SUPPORT WITH STEERING CONTROL SHAFTS - R+R

3

STEERING WHEEL ADJUSTMENT DEVICE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the condition of the steering wheel adjustment device.

In particular, check that the clearance between the device and the steering column is correct

Move on to Step 4

Coupling with excessive clearance.

Restore the efficiency of the steering wheel adjustment device.

If necessary, replace it

4

TIMING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the engine timing is correct.

Move on to Step 5

Timing not correct.

Restore correct engine timing

Op. 1032A12 TIMING SYSTEM DRIVE CHAIN - R.R

Op. 1032B10 TOOTHED TIMING BELT - R + R FOR TIMING ADJUSTMENT OR REPLACEMENT

5

FLYWHEEL BALANCING CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the flywheel is correctly balanced.

End of diagnosis

Balancing not correct.

Replace the flywheel

Op. 1024B10 ENGINE FLYWHEEL - R + R FOR VERSIONS WITH MANUAL GEARBOX
