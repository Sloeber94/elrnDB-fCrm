Noise, creaking

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

INTERFERENCE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for possible interference between the seat lifting adjustment components that could create noise/creaking (cover, lever, ratchet(s), linkage).

Move on to Step 2

Presence of noise/creaking through contact/interference between seat lifting components.

Move the parts in contact apart and, if necessary, lubricate the linkage where the creaking is located.

Do not lubricate the ratchet.

2

CHECK ON FRAME RATCHET ASSEMBLY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the ratchet(s) and frame are correctly assembled.

Move on to Step 3

Presence of noise between lever(s) and ratchet(s) and between ratchet(s) and frame as a result of loose fastening.

Retighten the badly fastened components to the correct torque or those with clearance.

3

CHECK THAT THERE IS NO NOISE FROM THE RATCHET

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for noise from the ratchet(s) and the frame in static and dynamic conditions (with or without the adjustment of the seat).

Move on to Step 4

1. Noise at the ratchet

1. Replace the ratchet(s)

Op. 7045A36 DRIVER'S SEAT MANUAL HEIGHT ADJUSTMENT DEVICE - R.R

2. Presence of noise at the linkage or at the torsion bar.

2. Lubricate parts in contact or renew welding.

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present.

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Replace the seat frame

Op. 7045A86 FRONT SEAT FRAME (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT, R.R.
