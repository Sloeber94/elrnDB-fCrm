Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle is equipped with a radio system comprising:

- radio;

- wiring (speakers and radio);

- speakers in the mirror triangle (tweeters);

- speakers in the front doors (mid-woofers);

- speakers in the rear door (full-range);

- AM/FM aerial coaxial cable;

- aerial mounting and stem.

In this configuration the radio is a CAN node through which information is exchanged with the Body Computer and the other electronic units, providing the customer with a series of functions, including:

- automatic switching off 20 minutes after the key off to protect the battery;

- control of the dimmed lighting in the radio display with the side lights on;

- volume control dependent on the vehicle speed (SVC);

- controls on the steering wheel (where fitted).

The radio, available in two different versions depending on customer requirements, can have a traditional CD player or CD MP3 player (compressed files).

The radio has a security system with a four digit identification code on a card that is handed over to the customer when the vehicle is received when there is a factory-fitted radio.

The above code should be entered every time the battery is disconnected to prevent it being reused on another vehicle.

On request a multipolar cable for connecting the radio with a CD Changer located in the oddments compartment on the passenger side can be fitted.

This configuration involves preparation for a mobile phone with hands-free kit.

Some versions are equipped with controls on the steering wheel (6 button version): these make it possible to replicate some of the commands which are acquired in the same way by the actual radio. The functions are:

- change/preset CD track or radio station (forwards);

- change/preset CD track or radio station (backwards);

- SCR (switch listening source);

- VOL+ (increase volume);

- VOL- (decrease volume);

- MUTE.
