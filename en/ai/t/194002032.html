O031 SEAT BELTS LOCKED

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the belt is fastened and with the vehicle parked, the belt locks easily and/or remains locked even when movements are not abrupt (e.g. picking up an object)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK REEL

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the reel is correctly fitted, ensuring that the reference pin is correctly positioned in its seat on the vehicle body.

Move on to Step 2

Incorrect installation

Loosen the bolt and position the reference dowel correctly. Retighten the bolt to the recommended torque.

2

CHECK FOR FOREIGN BODIES

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check there are no foreign bodies inside the system.

Move on to Step 3

Foreign bodies present.

Remove the foreign bodies.

3

CHECK BELTS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check for interference, deformation or dings caused by accidents.

Move on to Step 4

Interference present that makes the belts slide more slowly

Remove the interference and replace the damaged parts if necessary.

Presence of deformation or dings on the front anchorage link

Replace the front link

Op. 7065A24 ANCHORAGE LINK WITH PRETENSIONER FOR ONE FRONT SEAT BELT LH OR RH R R

Presence of deformation or dings on the rear anchorage link

Replace the rear link

Op. 7065B14 ANCHORAGE ROD FOR (ONE) REAR SEAT BELT LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Presence of deformations or dings on the front reel/pretensioner

Replace the front belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Presence of deformations or dings on the rear reel

Replace the rear seat belt

Op. 7065B08 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE) WITH PRETENSIONER ON REEL, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

4

CHECK PRETENSIONER OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that pretensioner has not be activated by an accident. In this case, the presence of white powder will be noticed on the spring case, the reel will be locked and/or the belt deteriorated.

Move on to Step 5

Pretensioner found to have activated.

Replace the belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

5

CHECK REEL RELEASE SYSTEM

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check reel release by rewinding the belt for a short section and then gently unwinding.

Move on to Step 6

Fault in the front reel release system

Replace the front belt

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

Fault in the rear reel release system

Replace the rear seat belt

Op. 7065B08 REAR SEAT BELT (ONE) WITH PRETENSIONER ON REEL, LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

6

CHECK CORRECT COMPONENT INSTALLATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check right/left component correspondence in relation to the side of the vehicle.

End of diagnosis

Incorrect installation

Restore correct installation and replace the parts if necessary
