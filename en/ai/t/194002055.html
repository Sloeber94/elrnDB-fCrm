S015 NOISE WHILST DRIVING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst driving, especially on unmade roads, there is an unspecific noise.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

This noise could be due to the engine assembly, the clutch-gearbox-differential unit or the braking system. In these cases the noise could also occur on even road surfaces or only in certain gears or certain conditions (gear engagement/release, braking, etc.)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Test results

1

CHECKS IN FRONT AREA OF PASSENGER COMPARTMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the noise is not due to the poor fastening of components or equipment. For example, check whether the noise could be caused by

  • Objects housed in the dashboard or in the front doors
  • Incorrect fastening of the front seat belts

Move on to Step 2

1.Noise due to objects in the various compartments

1.Fasten the objects better or wrap them in foam or a soft cloth to stop the noise

2.Seat belts incorrectly fastened

2.Renew the correct fastening

Op. 7065A10 FRONT SEAT BELT (ONE) LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R.

2

CHECKS IN THE LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the noise is not due to the poor fastening of components or equipment. For example, check whether the noise could be caused by

  • Objects housed in the luggage compartment
  • Spare wheel or tools (jack, etc.) incorrectly fastened
  • Incorrect fastening of the rear parcel shelf
  • Incorrect fastening of the rear boot

Move on to Step 3

1.Noise due to objects in the luggage compartment

1.Fasten the objects better or wrap them in foam or a soft cloth to stop the noise

2.Spare wheel or tools incorrectly fastened

2.Renew the correct fastening

3.Incorrect fastening of rear parcel shelf

3.Renew the correct fastening of the rear parcel shelf

Op. 7040H80 REAR PARCEL SHELF - R.R.

4.Incorrect fastening of rear boot

4.Restore the correct fastening of the rear boot

Op. 7210E56 R.R. TAILGATE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the noise is not due to the poor fastening of components or equipment. For example, check whether the noise could be caused by

  • Incorrect fastening of the rear seat

4

ROAD TEST TO IDENTIFY NOISE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Road test the vehicle to check the type of noise

See Test 70B ROAD TEST FOR NOISE RECOGNITION

End of diagnosis

1.The noise is due to systems other than the suspension (power unit-clutch-gearbox-differential-brakes-steering)

1.Search for the causes carrying out the appropriate fault diagnosis

2.The noise is due to interior or exterior creaking of dashboard components

2.Restore the correct fastening of the loose component

3.The noise is due to the sun roof

3.Search for the causes carrying out the appropriate fault diagnosis

4.The noise has not been isolated

4.Move on to step 5

5

CHECKS UNDER THE BODY

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check that the noise is not due to the poor fastening of components or equipment. For example, check whether the noise could be caused by

  • Incorrect fastening of the front liners or engine guards
  • Incorrect fastening of the rear liners or other exterior trim elements

End of diagnosis

Incorrect fastening of bodywork elements

Restore correct fastening of problem elements
