Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The anti-tilt sensor is located in the courtesy light module and is connected to the Body Computer by means of the dialogue line known as the A-BUS. The tilt sensor is operated and controlled by the Body Computer Node via a series of encrypted messages and can be excluded by the customer using the special button. If there is a failure the Body Computer Node detects and memorizes the causes of the malfunction

Applies to: all engines; all production periods
