Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Working from inside the car and using a Fein vibrating cutter and Fein cutting blade (63502103010, cut and remove the spoiler fastening area to free up the working area.

2. Working from inside the vehicle, cut the first sealant section (2a), cut the sealant (2b), still working from the rear, and remove the roof (2c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

1. Use appropriate equipment to cut and level the sealant strip to leave a thickness between 0.25 and 1 mm in the frame housing compartment.

The thickness of the sealant remaining in the frame housing will act as a support for the new bonding.

Thoroughly clean the frame housing compartment on the body with compressed air and degrease thoroughly with heptane

When bonding, 60 minutes after the bead of sealant has been cut and levelled, the surface of the actual sealant must be treated using the special activating product and it is necessary to wait for 15 minutes before fitting the windscreen to allow the primer thinner to evaporate.
