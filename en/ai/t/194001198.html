Removing

Refitting

1. Lower the grab handle (1a) and release and lift up the cover trims (1b).

2. Undo the fixing bolts and remove the grab handle from the vehicle.

Removing

- Check that the grab handle is not damaged.

- Position the grab handle and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Close the cover trims.
