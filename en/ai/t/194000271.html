1. Hydraulic tensioner in operational position.

Move the timing chain hydraulic tensioner to operating position by exercising overall pressure by hand on the chain in the area shown in the figure. This puts the chain under tension, which in turn acts on the mobile pad to release the mobile tensioner piston.When the tensioner piston is operated, the operating spring can be heard to click.
