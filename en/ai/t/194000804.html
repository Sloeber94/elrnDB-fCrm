- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

Refitting

1. Turn the steering wheel to gain access to the attachment points and then unscrew the bolts (1a) securing air bag module (1b) without fully removing them.

1. Remove the Air Bag module from the vehicle and place it in the special cabinet resting it on a free surface with the bag facing upwards.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

Observe the following precautions:

Handle the Air Bag module very carefully. Make sure that the battery is disconnected.

If a new module is being fitted, position the labels. Remove the detachable part of the adhesive label from the new module and place it in the special folder containing the vehicle details (model, registration date, chassis number) and the serial number of the old module. The file should be kept for future checks. A new adhesive plate comes together with the module and should be stuck on top of the existing one. Before sticking it it should be perforated at the month and 10 years after the year in which the module is being fitted (e.g. 2010 for 2000).

- Connect the electrical connection and do up the safety retainers.
