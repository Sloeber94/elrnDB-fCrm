While driving, the speedometer does not work or works irregularly.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Connect the Examiner equipment to the diagnostic socket.

Check that there are no faults in the instrument panel

Move on to Step 2

Presence of errors

Continue following the instructions for the diagnostic equipment

2

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Keeping the Examiner equipment connected to the diagnostic socket, check the operation of the pointer in active diganosis

Move on to Step 3

Speedometer not working

Replace instrument panel

Op. 5560B10 CONTROL PANEL - R+R

3

ELECTRICAL CIRCUIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check connections between instrument panel, Body Computer and ABS control unit

See E4010 INSTRUMENT PANEL

Move on to Step 4

1.Wiring defective

1.Repair/replace the wiring

2.ABS faulty

2.Restore ABS

See E7020 ABS

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the fault is no longer present

End of diagnosis
