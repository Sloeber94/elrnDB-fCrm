Refitting

- Open the front side door.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

2. Remove the panel (2a) after having raised it to release the upper profile (2b) from the door frame.

In order not to damage the panel, avoid pulling it before having released it from the retaining clips. Use leverage from the outside on the lower edge of the inner glass scraper starting from the bottom of the door until the panel is released from the glass scraper.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the panel is not damaged and check that the retaining clips are intact and correctly positioned.

- Attach the interior handle connecting rod.

- Position the deterrence led and tighten the bolts (only for the left front door).

- Connect the deterrence led electrical connection (only for the left front door).
