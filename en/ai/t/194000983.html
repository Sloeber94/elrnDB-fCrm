1. Release the blind (1a) from the drivers (1b) using a circular movement.

2. Using the special tool, release the end of travel pin (2a), undo the bolts (2b) and remove the brackets (2c) on both sides.

Removing the end of travel pins destroys them; use new replacement parts when refitting.

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the sunroof cable guide is not damaged.

- Position on the vehicle, tighten the fixing bolts and fit the pipes to the retaining clips along the entire length of the actual pipes.

- Fit the terminal mount to the pipes and tighten the bolts on both sides of the vehicle.

- Place the centre crossmember in the vehicle fitting the blind in the guides, tighten the fixing bolts to the sun roof frame and to the sun roof guides.

- Fit the mounting brackets and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Fit new end of travel pins and attach the blind to the drivers.
