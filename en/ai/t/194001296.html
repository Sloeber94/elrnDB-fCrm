Applies to: all engines; all production periods

talk unit H005 sends two earth signals, depending on left stalk wheel position, to the Body Computer M001:

rom pin 7 of connector C it sends a manual side light and low beam activation signal to pin 28 of connector G of the Body Computer.

- from pin 8 of connector C it sends an AUTO mode side light and low beam activation signal to pin 2 of connector G of the Body Computer (automatic dusk sensor activation).

Both signals are clearly incompatible with one another. If both signals are present, the lights are permanently off.

The side lights are activated in AUTO mode by the dusk sensor integral with the electrochrome interior mirror unit P065 (signal via the A-BUS serial line to pin 7 of connector D of M001).

See E1060 A-BUS SERIAL LINE

e dusk sensor is supplied from the INT line protected by fuse F49 of underfacia junction unit B002.

Body Computer M001 (pin 8 of connector F) controls the power supply to the dipped beams by sending an earth signal to underfacia junction unit B002 that excites relay T1: this supplies the low beams of headlamps F010 (left) and F011 (right). Each of the two lines is protected by a fuse in control unit B002:

- F13 for the left headlamp, from pin 3 connector A;

- F12 for the right headlamp, from pin 12 connector A.

The Follow me home function is activated by a low beam flasher earth signal sent from stalk unit H005 (pin 4 connector B) to Body Computer M001 (pin 33 connector G).
