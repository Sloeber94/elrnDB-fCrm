The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P2009 (flow valve).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if the control unit recognizes a malfunction in the circuit through an internal logic evaluation: with the ignition ON the switched power supply is more than 18 V or less than 9 V.
