Applies to: all engines; all production periods

- Undo the engine coolant reservoir cap and remove it.

- Fit the engine cooling system leakage test equipment on the reservoir filler.

Description

Function

Validity

1.895.377.001

Engine cooling system leakage test kit

Engine cooling system pressurization

-- Manually pressurize the circuit and check that the reading on the pressure gauge remains constant.

Measurement

Value

Validity

Engine cooling system leakage test pressure

1.08 bar

- Remove the tool from the engine coolant supply reservoir.

- Tighten the tool equipped with a special connector to the engine coolant supply reservoir cap.
