PRELIMINARY CHECKS

Operation

First of all check that the bonnet is correctly shut.

Check that the wind noise is actually coming from the bonnet.

1

CHECK ON LOCATION OF WIND NOISE FROM BONNET

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Carry out the test to locate the wind noise from the bonnet accurately

locate the area where the wind noise is coming from

Move on to Step 2

The wind noise is coming from the side area of the bonnet

Check the foam buffers where the bonnet and the wings are matched at the rear. Also check the condition of wind seals and make sure they are fitted correctly (see

Op. 7025A62 BONNET LID WEATHERSTRIP - R+R

The wind noise is coming from the area of the grille

Check for the seal between the bonnet and the radiator grille

Op. 7055A84 FRONT BADGE ON BONNET LID OR RADIATOR BADGE - R.R.

The wind noise is coming from the front area of the bonnet.

Check the condition of the front wind seal and make sure it is fitted correctly

Op. 7025A62 BONNET LID WEATHERSTRIP - R+R

2

FLAME PARTITION CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check the flame partition. In particular, check:

- the plugs are present;

- the plugs are correctly fitted;

- the bulkhead coupling rubber fittings are installed correctly;

- sealant properly applied;

- correct installation.

End of diagnosis

Fireproof bulklhead faulty

Renew fireproof bulkhead
