Refitting

- Position the driveshaft in a vice fitted with protective jaws.

1. Remove the bands securing the protective boot using a suitable tool.

1. Retract the protective boot on the driveshaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Clean the driveshaft constant velocity joint until the safety ring is accessible.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Extend the safety ring using suitable pliers.

2. Extract the wheel side constant velocity joint from the driveshaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Remove the wheel side constant velocity joint safety ring.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

1. Remove the protective boot.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Wash the constant velocity joint components thoroughly using diesel oil or petrol and visually inspect that there are no signs of seizing or grooves on the balls or seats.

- Fit a new protective boot on the driveshaft.

1. Place a new safety ring in the housing on the constant velocity joint.

Check that the safety ring is correctly fitted.

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2; all production periods

- Place the wheel side constant velocity join in its housing on the driveshaft.

Push the constant velocity joint until the safety ring is correctly positioned.

- Fill the joint and the boot with the specified grease.

- Position the driveshaft in a vice fitted with protective jaws.

1. Remove the bands securing the protective boot using a suitable tool.

2. Turn the boot upsidedown to uncover the three-lobe joint.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1. Use an appropriate drift (1a) and mallet to disconnec the inner differential drive shaft (1b) from the drive shaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

1. Remove the circlip securing the three-lobed joint.

2. Use an appropriate drift (2a) and mallet to remove the three lobed joing (2b) from the front drive shaft.

- Remove the gaiter from the front drive shaft.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Use an appropriate fitting tool to install the three-lobed joint in its seat on the drive shaft.

- Place a new circlip in position to secure the three-lobed joint.

- Pack the joint and the boot with the specified grease.
