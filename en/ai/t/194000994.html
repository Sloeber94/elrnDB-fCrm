Working on both sides of the vehicle:

1. Undo the blind fixing bolts (1a) and the bolts (1b) fixing the crossmember (1c).

2. Partly release the pipes (2a) and (2b) for the cable guides from the retaining clips.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Release the cable loom from the retaining clip.

2. Undo the bolts (2a) fixing the cable guide (2b).

3. Undo the fixing bolt (3a) carefully lower the cable guide and remove the crossmember (3b) extracting it as illustrated by the arrow.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the crossmember in position, tighten the fixing bolts to the chassis and the fixing bolts for the blind.

- Fit the pipes on the retaining clips and tighten the bolts fixing the cable guides.

- Position the cable loom and fit it.
