Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: RIGHT HAND DRIVE

The vehicle is equipped with a radio system comprising:

- radio;

- wiring (radio and speakers);

- speakers on the mirror triangle (tweeters);

- speakers on the front doors (mid-woofers);

- speakers on the rear doors (full-range);

- AM/FM aerial coaxial lead;

- aerial mount and stem.

In this configuration, the radio is a node on the CAN network, via which it exchanges information with the Body Computer and the other electronic units to provide the customer with a set of functions, namely:

- automatic deactivation 20 minutes after Key-OFF to safeguard the battery;

- control of dimmed radio display lighting with the side lights on;

- vehicle speed dependent volume control (SVC);

- steering wheel controls (where applicable)

The radio is available in two different versions according to customer needs and may be fitted with a conventional CD or a MP3 (compressed file) CD player.

The radio is equipped with a safety system with a four-figure identification code on a card given to the customer at the time the vehicle is collected with a radio fitted as part of its original equipment.

The above code should be entered whenever the battery is disconnected to prevent its reuse on another vehicle.

It is also possible to fit a multi-polar cable connecting the radio to a CD changer located in the glove compartment on the passenger side.

This configuration includes wiring for a mobile phone with handsfree kit.

Some versions are equipped with radio controls on the steering wheel (6 key version): these make it possible to replicate some of the commands which are acquired as analogue signals by the radio. The following functions are present:

- CD track or radio station change/present (forward);

- CD track or radio station change/present (back);

- SCR (change source);

- VOL+ (increase volume);

- VOL- (decrease volume);

- MUTE.
