Removing

Refitting

1. Raise the button (1a) until the retaining tooth (1b) can be releaased.

Applies to: engines: 2.2, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Proceed gradually whilst lifting the button. Lifting it too much could cause the pin (1c) and the spring (1d) to come out with it then being impossible to refit the button correctly. This condition is highlighted by the impossibility of placing the lever in P.

Applies to: engines: 2.2, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

To prevent the button from being lifted accidentally during the later stages, fit a piece of adhesive tape to secure the button to the grip.

1. Release and remove the frame for the boot (1b) from the housing and lift it as necessary to disconnect the electrical connection (1b).

Description

Connector

1b

Automatic transmission gear selector control unit

See M053 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION GEAR SELECTOR CONTROL UNIT

2. Open the ring securing the boot to the gear lever.

3. Remove the automatic transmission lever grip (3a) complete with boot (3b) extracting it upwards.

Once extracted, press the button to return it into position, then remove the adhesive tape.

Applies to: engines: 2.2, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

If a new grip is being fitted, the button is supplied by the Parts Dept. in the closed position.

- Place the selector lever in position R.

- Fit the automatic transmission lever grip complete with boot, keeping the lever in position R with the button in the closed position.

- Position a new ring securing the boot to the gear lever and close it.

- Connect the electrical connection and place the boot surround in position.

Check that the lever is working properly and is correctly position, especially in P.
