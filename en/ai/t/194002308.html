0010T21 ENGINE COOLANT - CHECK DENSITY AND CHANGE, IF NECESSARY

Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2, 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

- When the engine is cold, check that the level of coolant in the reservoir reaches the KALT COLD notch on the reservoir.

- If necessary, top up with the specified fluid to the "KALT COLD" notch.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is hot as there is a risk of scalding.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

- When the engine is cold, check that the level of coolant in the reservoir reaches the "Max" reference on the actual reservoir.

- If necessary, top up with the specified fluid to the "Max." reference.

Do not remove the tank cap when the engine is hot as there is a risk of scalding.
