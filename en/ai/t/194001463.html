Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The "boot open" signal is sent by the switch - located in the handle I033 - to pin 35 of connector E of the Body Computer M001.

The operation of the boot opening geared motor N057 takes place by means of the remote control signal: in this case, the ignition device H003, receives the signal and transmits it via the CAN to the Body Computer M001 (pins 24 and 25 of connector H).

The boot lock geared motor N057 receives the activation signal from pin 11 of connector F of the Body Computer M001.

The geared motor N057 receives a power supply until the opening of the boot is detected: the "boot not properly shut" signal is, in effect, sent by switch I011 which is connected to pin 24 of connector E.

The contact in question signals the "boot not properly shut" condition; the switch is fitted with another contact that signals the "boot open" condition, a contact used by the boot light function

See E2520 GLOVE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - LUGGAGE COMPARTMENT LIGHT - SUN VISOR LIGHT

The driver's door control unit M066 receives the door locking/unlocking signals from the controls on the tunnel H036 - pins 34 and 35 of connector A.

On the basis of the signal received, the fuel flap lock geared motor N058 is operated by the driver's door control unit M066 via pins 12 and 5 of connector D.
