Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

Work carefully on the fittings and use the recommended tools correctly to prevent damage.

Use the specialist equipment for removing the window glasses, working very carefully to avoid damaging the glass and the bodyshell.

During the operations illustrated below always use either the piano wire or the vibrating blade to cut the sealant. The two tools can usually be used alternately.

Follow the manufacturer's recommendations for sealants meticulously as far as the instructions for usage and times are concerned.

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition key OFF and DISCONNECT THE BATTERY.

Use the specialist equipment for removing the window glasses, working very carefully to avoid personal injury.

Always move the glass with the help of an assistant.
