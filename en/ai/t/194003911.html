Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

At operating temperature, with the engine idling, the reading should be between 10 and 120°C. The test may be said to be concluded

Move on to Step 2

2

Press the START/STOP button and turn off the engine.

Disconnect the cable loom connector from the temperature sensor and fuel heating resistance

See K100 TEMPERATURE SENSOR AND HEATING RESISTANCE

Press the START/STOP button.

Measure the voltage between the sensor Pin 4 and Earth. Ensure is between 4.8 and 5.2 V. Go on to Step 4

See K100 TEMPERATURE SENSOR AND HEATING RESISTANCE

Move on to Step 3

3

If faults are present, repair or if necessary replace the electrical wiring between the engine management control unit and the resistance

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

The test can be considered over

Replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R

4

Measure the voltage between Pins 3 and 4 of the sensor. Check it is between 4.8 and 5.2 V

See K100 TEMPERATURE SENSOR AND HEATING RESISTANCE

Move on to Step 3

5

Replace the diesel heating resistance and ensure the fault has disappeared

Op. 1044D10 FUEL HEATER - R + R

Repair the original heater coil and replace the engine management control unit

Op. 1060G80 DIESEL ELECTRONIC INJECTION CONTROL UNIT - .R
