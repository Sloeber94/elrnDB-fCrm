Removing

Refitting

Before removing and refitting the Air Bags the following preliminary operations must be carried out.

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position and extract it.

- Disconnect the negative and positive battery terminals and insulate them.

- Wait for 10 minutes before continuing.

Removing

- Check that the control motor for the air conditioning air distribution flap is not damaged.

- Place the air distribution flap control motor in position.

- Tighten the two bolts fixing the air distribution flap control motor to the air conditioning casing and secure it.

- Connect the electrical connection to the distribution flap control motor.
