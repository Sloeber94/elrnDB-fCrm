Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The vehicle is available, on request, with wiring for installing a cell phone.

The system includes:

- connection for connecting a cell phone with, as an option, hands-free function via the radio speakers;

- external GSM aerial.

the microphone to complement the hands-free function is not included because it comes with the kit for connecting the telephone and should be positioned freely.

The connection for the telephone, located at the base of the centre console, includes all the power supplies and signals needed for the operation of the telephone.

As an option, steering wheel controls are available with a 6-key configuration: these are used to send out radio and also phone commands.

the aerial mount and rod is fitted on the rear of the roof: it includes two cables (dual-function aerial). One is for the radio aerial, the other is the phone cable. To sum up, the functions provided are Radio and GSM.

The radio receives input audio signals from the telephone connection; the telephone sound is always received at a fixed volume which can, however, be adjusted in the radio menu function. The operation of the telephone switches off the radio temporarily (MUTE function).

The phone connection has two power supplies: one direct with key turned ON in the ignition. the above two lines are protected by two fuses in the junction unit under the dashboard; there is also a power supply with the side lights on (lights +) to light up the telephone, if fitted.
