S017 FRONT SUSPENSION NOISE (WHISTLING/HUMMING)

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Whilst driving, especially on unmade roads, there is a whistling noise coming from the front suspension. This diagnosis can only be made after

See table S015 VEHICLE RUNNING NOISE

Carry out the latter first.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

If the problem is only on one side, carry out the checks and any operations on the appropriate side only.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Having carried out the road test, during the

See table S015 VEHICLE RUNNING NOISE

, there was a whistling noise from the front.

Continue depending on the circumstances in which the noise occurs.

End of diagnosis

The noise increases when cornering.

Move on to Step 1.

The noise increases in vertical oscillation conditions.

Move on to Step 2.

1

CHECKS ON WHEEL KNUCKLE

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Visually inspect that the yield point type bolts fixing the bearing to the steering knuckle have not loosened.

Move on to Step 2

Shear bolts loose.

Replace the bolts and restore the correct tightening.

2

BRAKE DISC CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Check for any distortion to the disc heat shield.

Turn the wheel assembly to check for noise due to contact between disc and heat shield.

Move on to Step 3

Heat shield deformed.

Restore the correct gap

Op. 3310A59 FRONT BRAKE DISC GUARD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

Replace the shield if necessary

Op. 3310A59 FRONT BRAKE DISC GUARD (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT- R.R

Contact between heat shield and disc.

Replace the brake disc.

Op. 3310A48 FRONT BRAKE DISC (ONE), LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R

if the noise persists, replace the wheel bearing with the hub

Op. 4450C28 FRONT WHEEL HUB,LEFT OR RIGHT - R.R WITH WHEEL REMOVED, CHECK WHEEL GEOMETRY SEPARATELY

3

SHOCK ABSORBER STEM CHECK

All OK

Problems encountered

Operation

Remove the left wheel

Op. 4450B02 LEFT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

and/or the right one

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

Check that the noise is not due to irregular friction between the shock absorber stem and the gasket, checking the condition (scoring/contact marks) of the stem chrome surface.

End of diagnosis

There is irregular friction

Lubricate the stem raising the dust cover with a layer of silicon grease.

If the noise persists, replace the shock absorber
