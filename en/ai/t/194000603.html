Refitting

Removing

- Check that the condenser is not damaged.

- Place the condenser in the vehicle and fit the two lower mountings in their housings.

- Tighten the two upper bolts fixing the condenser.

- Position the air duct on the vehicle and tighten the four fixing bolts.

- Tighten the nut fastening the upper connecting pipe to the condenser and tighten to the recommended torque.
