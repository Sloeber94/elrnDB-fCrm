I023 HIGH BEAM (ONE) RH OR LH DOES NOT COME ON OR COMES ON IRREGULARLY

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

The halogen bulb with the main beam function is working properly, whilst the bixenon bulb does not switch from dipped beam to main beam.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1

CHECK USING DIAGNOSTIC EQUIPMENT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no errors by connecting to the Master (left side) or Slave (right side) headlamp using the diagnostic equipment

End of diagnosis

Error B1060 detected - Gas discharge headlamp actuator

Move on to Step 2

2

CHECK ON CONNECTORS IN BALLAST CONTROL UNIT

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the 21-way connector for the headlamp is correctly fitted in the housing in the ballast control unit.

Check that the tree-way connector - with two wires (inside the headlamp) is correctly fitted with the coil connector that controls the main beam function, by carrying out the test.

See Test 5540BP CHECK ON MAIN BEAM HEADLAMPS COIL POWER SUPPLY

Move on to Step 3

3

CHECK ON ACTUATOR FOR MAIN BEAM FUNCTION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the left headlamp

Op. 5540B81 LEFT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

or the right one

Op. 5540B82 RIGHT GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER - R.R.

(the faulty one) and check that the actuator is working properly

End of diagnosis

Actuator not working

Refit the headlamp replaced previously and move on to step 4

4

CHECK ON CORRECT OPERATION

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Replace the headlamp ballast control unit

Op. 5540B42 CONTROL UNIT OF ONE GAS DISCHARGE LIGHT CLUSTER R R

End of diagnosis

The fault persists

Contact technical service for further information on the problem
