Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and remove the left flange on the differential (1b).

The oil seal is built into the flange and cannot be replaced on its own.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Moisten a new shaft oil seal with transmission fluid.

1. Position the left flange in its housing in the differential (1a) and secure it tightening the bolts (1b) to the recommended torque.
