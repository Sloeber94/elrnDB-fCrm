Removing

Refitting

1. Extract the belt (1a) upwards to access the fixing bolts and make sure that during the entire removing-refitting procedure the sliding locking button (1b) does not enter the seat.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the bolts (1a) and release the trim (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Remove the trim releasing the belt (1a) via the opening (1b).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Make sure that the belt sliding locking button does not enter the seat.

- Insert the belt via the opening in the trim.

- Position the trim in the housing and tighten the bolts.
