Removing

Refitting

- Open the bonnet.

Op. 7210E20 R.R. BONNET

1. Position the bonnet lid on a stand.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Use an electric drill with a bit of appropriate size to remove the moulding rivets.

Recover the remains of the rivets.

2. Proceeding with caution in order not to damage the bonnet lid, remove the moulding from its seat.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the moulding does not display signs of damage.

- P osition the moulding its seat on the bonnet lid and secure with rivets of appropriate size.
