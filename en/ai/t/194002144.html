Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolt (1a) loosened previously completely and remove the mobile timing belt tensioner (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Reposition the moving timing system tensioner in its housing and secure it without tightening the bolt.

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

- On this version it is not possible to remove/refit the mobile timing belt tensioner on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then work at the bench, customising the engine service procedures for this operation.

Applies to: engines: 1.8; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Undo the bolt (1a) and remove the moving tensioner (1b).

Applies to: engines: 1.8; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the moving tensioner in position taking care to fit the free end of the spring in the special groove in the crankshaft oil seal cover.

- Tighten the moving tensioner retaining bollt to the recommended torque.
