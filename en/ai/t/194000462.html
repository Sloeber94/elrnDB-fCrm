The following procedure describes removal/refitting of the right drive shaft, do the same for the left drive shaft.

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

Op. 4450B04 RIGHT FRONT WHEEL - R.R

1. Remove front hub cap (1a) using the tool (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Remove the right drive shaft from the intermediate drive shaft and remove.

2. Remove the safety ring on the intermediate shaft using a screwdriver.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Refit a new safety ring in its seat on the intermediate shaft.

- Lubricate the intermediate drive shaft and right drive shaft mating surfaces with transmission fluid.

- Connect the right driveshaft to the intermediate driveshaft.

Fit the drive shaft until the safety ring locks. Take care not to spoil the teeth.

- Refit the drive shaft in its seat on the wheel hub and secure by screwing in a new nut without tightening.

1. Connect lower wishbone (1a) to the right front stub axle and tighten a new bolt (1b) to the specified torque.
