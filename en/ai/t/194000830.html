Applies to: all engines; all production periods

WARNINGS

Observe the following precautions when working on the front door assembly:

- If working on the hinges, take care not to damage the paintwork.

- After an adjustment that has involved moving the hinges, the door must be removed and the paint in the area of the hinges retouched.

- Work carefully on the fitting components and, to avoid damage, use the recommended tools correctly.

PRECAUTIONS

Before working on the electrical system (connectors, electrical components, wiring, etc.) turn the ignition OFF and disconnect the battery.
