Removing

Refitting

1. Remove the sound insulation cover fastened to the engine by three pins.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Loosen the band and disconnect the coolant pipe from the cylinder head, cylinder head side.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

1. Undo the nut (1a), loosen the band and remove the coolant pipe from the cylinder head (1b).

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the coolant pipe from the cylinder head in its housing, then tighten the bands and the nut.

- Place the sound insulation cover back in its housing and secure it to the engine using the three pins.
