Op. 2130A10 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION - R + R

On this vehicle in order to remove-refit the automatic transmission, the power unit must be removed-refitted.Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + Rand then separated at the bench.Op. 1004D20 POWER UNIT (AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION) REMOVED, REMOVE AND RE-BUILDThe latter operation includes removal/refitting of the automatic gearbox.

Applies to: engines: 2.2; all production periods

Removing

Op. 2130A10 AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION - R + R

Applies to: engines: 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

- On this version it is not possible to remove/refit the automatic transmission torque converter on the car.

To perform this operation, remove/refit the power unit

Op. 1004B14 POWER UNIT WITH AUTOMATIC TRANSMISSION R + R

and then work at the bench, customising the engine service procedures for this operation.
