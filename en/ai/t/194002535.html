Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: MANUAL AIR CONDITIONING

Disconnect the electrical connector for the climate control system control unit

See M70 CLIMATE CONTROL SYSTEM CONTROL UNIT

With the ignition ON, turn the temperature knob to the cold position (completely to the left). After about 10 secs. turn the knob to the hot position (completely to the right) checking that the voltage between pins 4 and 5 is between 9.6 and 14.4 V whilst the motor is turning. Repeat the manoeuvre in the opposite direction. Also check that the voltage between pins 29 and 8 is between 4.75 and 5.25 V.
