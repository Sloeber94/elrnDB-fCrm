Applies to: all engines; all production periods; only with equipment: ESP

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check power supply (+15)

  • Connect using the Examiner in Voltmeter mode to the positive terminal at pin 5 and the negative terminal of the steering angle sensor earthed.
  • Turn the key to the on position and read the battery voltage of around 12 volt

Move on to Step 2

Check the continuity of fuse F42 and if broken replace it checking out the cause.

2

Check earth connection

Disconnect the sensor connector.

Connect using an ohmmeter between pin 6 of the sensor and an earth and check that the resistance is less than 1 ohm

Restore the continuity of the earth connection (dashboard area, right side)
