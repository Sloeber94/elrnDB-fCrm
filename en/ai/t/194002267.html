5520AA IGNITION SWITCH EFFICIENCY CHECK

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Inser the key in the ignition and turn to STOP

Check that the key is released mechanically in the ignition.

Check that the steering is released.

Restore B-Can continuity

See E1050 CAN CONNECTION LINES

2

Connect the diagnostic tester to the TEG Reader node.

Insert the key in the ignition and check that the system recognises the key by turning ON (transponder reading stage).

The test can be considered over

Replace the key and store it in the Teg Reader node.

See descriptions 5580E ANTI-THEFT DEVICE

Move on to Step 3

3

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive terminal to Pin 3 and the negative earthed to Pin 4 of the ignition device

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

The direct voltage reading should be 12 Volt.

Restore the power supply

See E5010 STARTING AND RECHARING

4

Fit the key in the ignition.

Connect the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode with the positive terminal connected to device Pin 12 and the negative earthed. The direct battery voltage reading should be less than 10.6 Volts.

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Replace the ignition lock

Op. 5520A10 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R+R

5

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to device Pin 6 and the negative to earth

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Turn the key ON and check for a voltage of 12 Volts.

Replace the ignition lock

Op. 5520A10 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R+R

6

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to device Pin 5 and the negative to earth

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Press the clutch pedal.

Turn the key ON and check for a voltage of 12 Volts.

Replace the ignition lock

Op. 5520A10 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R+R

7

With the diagnostic instrument in Voltmeter mode, connect the positive probe to device Pin 11 and the negative to earth

See H003 IGNITION DEVICE

Press the clutch pedal.

Turn the key ON and check for a voltage of 12 Volts.

Replace the ignition lock

Op. 5520A10 IGNITION SWITCH ASSEMBLY - R+R
