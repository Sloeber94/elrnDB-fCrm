SPECIFICATIONS

The main specifications of the 2.2 engine are as follows:

- four cylinder in-line arrangement;

- dual overhead camshaft with 16 valve timing system;

- Motorola E16 injection system

- cylinder block, cylinder head, oil sump and cam cover in aluminium alloy;

- cylinder liners dry press-fitted into the engine block;

- hydraulic tappets;

- two chain-driven balancing countershafts;

- internal water pump;

- oil filter built into the engine block;

- chain-driven timing system (maintenance-free);

- ignition module with built-in spark plug connectors;

- EOBD;

- two Lambda sensors.
