For the correct operation of the Multijet versions with DPF, only use the original type of lubricant. In case of emergency, where the original product is not available, only top up with a maximum of 0.5 litres.If SAE 5W-40 non original products are used, lubricants with minimum ACEA A3 standards for petrol engines are acceptable, ACEA B4 for diesel engines; in this case the optimum engine performance is not guaranteed.The use of products with lower specifications than ACEA A3 and ACEA B4 could cause damage to the engine not covered by the warranty.(•) SELENIA DIGITECH 0W-30 is recommended to optimise start-up performance under harsh weather conditions
