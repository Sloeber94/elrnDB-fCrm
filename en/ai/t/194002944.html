The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0237 (Turbo pressure)

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if the difference between the actual distribution line oil pressure and the nominal pressure is more than 250 bar for at least 2 secs.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the engine oil level is correct

Op. 0010T11 ENGINE OIL - CHECK LEVEL AND TOP UP, IF NECESSARY

Check that the air intake/vacuum circuit is working properly by carrying out the

See Test 1048OA AIR INTAKE/VACUUM CIRCUIT CHECK

Check the condition of the fuel supply circuit and make sure that it is working properly carrying out the

See Test 1040OA FUEL SUPPLY CIRCUIT CHECK

Visually inspect the condition of:

  • High pressure fuel pump
  • Injector pipe
  • Fuel pressure sensor on the rail
  • Fuel pressure regulator
  • Fuel shutter solenoid valve
  • Injectors

ACTION

LOW RETURN PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the pressure in the return pipe to the tank is less than 1 bar

See Test 1060OA CHECK ON RETURN LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

Move on to Step 5

Pressure above the maximum limit: return pipe to the tank obstructed

Repair the pipe, if necessary, replace it

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

Move on to Step 6

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Constrict the return pipe downstream of the valve and carry out the test again

See Test 1060OB CHECK ON SUPPLY LOW PRESSURE FUEL CIRCUIT

with the pipe constricted checking that the pressure at the filter outlet remains below 2 bar

LOW SUPPLY PRESSURE CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the pump supply voltage is equal to 12V and that the current uptake is between 2 and 10 A

See Test 1040AA SUPPLY VOLTAGE AND ABSORPTION CURRENT CHECKS

Move on to Step 11

1. Voltage lower than 12 V

1. Renew electrical wiring

See E5050 DIESEL ENGINES ELECTRONIC MANAGEMENT

2. Current greater than 10 A: pump defective

2. Replace the fuel pump

3. Current less than 2 A: pump obstructed

3. Replace the fuel pump and clean the fuel tank
