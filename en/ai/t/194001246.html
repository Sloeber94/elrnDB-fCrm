Removing

Refitting

- Turn the ignition key to the OFF position.

- Open the bonnet.

- Disconnect the negative battery terminal.

Description

Connector

BATTERY

See A001 BATTERY

1. Undo the bolts (1a) fixing the check strap (1b) to the pillar and close the door so that the check strap is inside.

2. Disconnect the electrical connection.
