SPECIFICATIONS

This engine is fitted with a high pressure electronic injection system for fast diesel engines. This Bosch Common Rail EDC 16 C39 system is fully managed by an electronic control unit.

To reduce polluting emission levels, this engine is equipped with:

- exhaust system with oxidizing catalytic converter and DPF system (particle filter)

- exhaust gas recirculation system E.G.R.

- crankcase blow-by gas recirculation system.
