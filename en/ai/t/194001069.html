Refitting

Removing

- Pas the front seat belt through the slot on the central pillar trim upper element.

- Position the central pillar trim upper element in its seat and secure to the body, tightening the bolts.

- Reposition the cover located in the upper part of the trim.

- Place the seat belt in its seat and tighten the fixing bolt.

- Fit the front seat belt anchorage guard to the seat.
