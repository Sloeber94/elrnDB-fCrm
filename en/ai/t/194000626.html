Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Removing

Refitting

1. Release the seal from the air intake vent and remove it.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Disconnect the electrical connection from the air recirculation flap control motor.

Description

Connector

1

OUTSIDE AIR/RECIRCULATION INTAKE FLAP ACTUATOR

See N082 OUTSIDE AIR/RECIRCULATION INTAKE FLAP ACTUATOR

2. Release the wiring (2a) from the mountings, undo the fixing bolts (2b) and remove the intake air and recirculation flap (2c) from the air conditioning casing.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Check that the intake air and recirculation flap assembly is not damaged.

- Place the intake air and recirculation flap assembly on the climate control casing, check that the assembly is correctly matched and tighten the fixing bolts.

- Connect the electrical connection to the air recirculation flap control motor.

Description

Connector

OUTSIDE AIR/RECIRCULATION INTAKE FLAP ACTUATOR

See N082 OUTSIDE AIR/RECIRCULATION INTAKE FLAP ACTUATOR

- Attach the wiring to the mountings.
