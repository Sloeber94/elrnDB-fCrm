The diagnostic equipment signals: Error P0111 (flow meter air temp. signal).

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods

This error is detected if there is interference with the signal for the sensor at Pin 37 of the electronic control module (detail "general electrical fault") or if there is a short circuit to positive (detail "beyond the upper limit") or to earth (detail "below the lower limit") in the circuit connected to Pin 37 of the control unit. In the case of short circuits, the control unit takes 25 °C as the replacement value.

Applies to: engines: 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v, 2.4 JTD 20v; all production periods
