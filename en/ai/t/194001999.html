J005 BACK WINDOW WASHER IS NOT WORKING

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

When the lever on the right of the dashboard is pushed towards it, the rearscreen washer function is not activated properly, but the rearscreen wiper is working

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

0

PRELIMINARY CHECKS

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

First of all check the level of the detergent fluid in the reservoir and also make sure that it is not leaking

Move on to Step 1

1. Insufficient detergent fluid level.

1. Restore the correct level.

Op. 5050A14 RESERVOIR PLUG (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) OR WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID LEVEL SENSOR - CHECK AND TOP UP IF NECESSARY

2. Detergent fluid reservoir damaged.

2. Replace the reservoir

Op. 5050A10 WINDSCREEN WASHER FLUID RESERVOIR (IN ENGINE COMPARTMENT) - R+R

1

PIPE AND JET CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that there are no obstructions in the rearscreen washer pipes and that they are not incorrectly connected.

Check the condition of the jet.

Move on to Step 2

1. Pipes blocked or damaged

1. Repair or replace the pipes

2. Jet obstructed or damaged.

2. Repair or replace the jet

Op. 5050C10 REAR WINDOW WASHER JET - R+R CLEAN AND/OR POSITION

2

REARSCREEN WASHER ELECTRIC PUMP POWER SUPPLY

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the connection between the steering column switch unit and the rearscreen washer pump

See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

Move on to Step 3

Connection broken.

Repair the connections

See E3020 WINDSCREEN WASH/WIPE

3

REARSCREEN WASHER PUMP CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check the operation of the rearscreen washer electric pump

Move on to Step 4

It is the actual pump that carries out the screen washer function but operated in the reverse direction

Pump faulty.

Replace the screen washer electric pump

Op. 5050B22 FRONT AND REAR SCREEN WASHER PUMP - R+R

4

STEERING COLUMN SWITCH UNIT CHECK

ALL OK

PROBLEMS ENCOUNTERED

ACTION

Check that the stalk unit is working properly: in particular the rearscreen wiper switch

See descriptions 5550 WARNING AND MANOEUVRING INDICATORS

End of diagnosis

Stalk unit not working properly.

Replace the steering column switch unit.

Op. 5550A10 STALK UNIT ASSEMBLY - R+R
