Applies to: all engines; all production periods

STEP

CHECK TO BE PERFORMED

RESOLUTION IF THE CHECK IS NOT OK

1

Check the direct power supply

Check that a direct voltage of +12 is present at pin C8 of the subwoofer audio amplifier

Replace the 30 Amp fuse F54 in the under-dashboard fuse control unit.

2

Check the earth

Check that pin C1 is correctly earthed

Restore the earth connection

3

Check the ignition enablement

Check with the key on and Connect on that a 12 Volt signal is present at pin C2

Restore the wiring across pin C2 of the amplifier and pin C6 of the radio

4

Check status recognition

With Connect on, check that a 12 Volt signal is present at pin C3.

Restore the wiring across pin C3 of the amplifier and pin A2 of the radio
