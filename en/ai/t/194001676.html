Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2, 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods; only with equipment: RIGHT HAND DRIVE

The air conditioning is electric manual type with the distribution featuring the possibility of adjusting and directing the flows in many ways, thereby guaranteeing an even effect throughout the passenger compartment.

An electronic control unit manages the passenger compartment climate control and adjusts air distiribution and mixing by means of actuators.

The control for different fan speeds is via a resistance partitioner protected against temperature surges by a thermal fuse and two relays.

All versions are equipped with a pollen filter and air distribution to the rear passengers.

The manual climate control layout features three rotary controls and 4 buttons.

- the left button adjusts distribution;

- the central button adjusts temperature;

- the right button adjusts fan speed;

The panel contains the buttons for switching on the compressor, recirculation, MAX DEF and the heated rear windscreen.

The following parameters/functions can be altered manually:

- temperature;

- distribution in 5 positions;

- fan speed;

- compressor enablement;

- defrosting/demisting function;

- recirculation.

The right central area of the panel houses the air recirculation buttons and the air conditioner compressor activation button.

See E6021 COMPRESSOR ENGAGEMENT

The left central part houses the heated rear window and windscreen demist controls.

See E3030 HEATED REAR WINDSCREEN AND DOOR MIRROR DEFROSTING

The manual climate control ECU is supplied by two lines, one direct and the other ignition-operated. These are protected by two fuses in the under-dashboard junction unit.

The fan receives a power supply from a line protected by a special fuse and operated by a specific relay in the engine compartment junction unit.
