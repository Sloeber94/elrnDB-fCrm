Refitting

- Position the vehicle on a lift.

- Make sure that the ignition key is in the OFF position, then disconnect the battery terminal (-).

- Remove the protective cap from the bleed valve on the brake caliper.

- Connect the recovery device (pipe and reservoir) to the brake caliper bleed valve.

- Loosen the bleed valve on the brake caliper and wait for the brake-clutch reservoir and pipes to drain.

1. Undo the connector (1a) and disconnect the rigid pipe from the connector to the left rear brake (1b), flexible pipe side.

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

1. Undo the connector (1a), disconnect the rigid pipe from the connector to the left rear brake (1b), intermediate rigid pipe side and remove it after having released it from the underbody fastenings (1c).

Applies to: all engines; all production periods

Refitting

Removing

- Place the rigid pipe from the connector to the left rear brake back in its housing and secure it using the underbody fastenings.

- Connect the rigid pipe from the connector to the left rear brake, intermediate rigid pipe side and tighten the connector to the recommended torque.
