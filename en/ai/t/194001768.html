Applies to: engines: 1.8, 2.2, 1.9 JTD 8v, 1.9 JTD 16v; all production periods

The steering lock control unit M089 controls and regulates the entire electrically controlled steering column locking system.

The direct power supply for the control unit M089 - pin C1 - arrives from the battery via the line protected by fuse F3 of the engine compartment junction unit B001. Pin A4 of M089 is earthed.

The steering lock control unit M089, depending on the rotation of the key in the ignition device H003, receives the following signals:

- at pin A6, the key inserted signal (TEG_IN);

- at pin B5, the key turned to ON signal (TEG_MAR);

- at pin C5, the first starting signal with the key turned to START (TEG_Crank1);

- at pin C6, the second starting signal with the key turned to START (TEG_Crank2);

The ignition device H003 - pins 1 and 2 - communicates, via the CAN, with the steering lock control unit M089 - pins A2 and A3 - for the enablement of the steering release after recognizing the TEG inserted in the reader.

At pin A5 the steering lock control unit M089 receives the earth signal for the clutch pedal I031 pressure.

In recovery conditions, the control unit M089 - pin C4 - uses the NA contact signal for the switch on the brake pedal I030.

The steering lock control unit M089, depending on the position of the key in the ignition device H003, provides the following power supplies:

- INT, from pin A1 to the junction unit under the dashboard B002 - pin 11 of connector H -;

- INT/A, from pin C8 to the junction unit under the dashboard B002 - pin 1 of connector H -;

- 15/54, from pin B1 to the engine compartment junction unit B001 - pin 30 of connector A - ;

- +50, from pin B8 to the starter motor A020.
